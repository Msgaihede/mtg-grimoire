### Highlights

- **You can now empty a whole deck list at once.** A new Empty a list section at the foot of Deck settings clears the live list or the theory list in one press. Copies you own from a live list go to Recently removed.
- **Card view has quantity controls on the collection and wishlist.** Card view now has plus and minus controls for copies in the collection and for wants on the wishlist, as list view already did.
- **Stepping a wish down to zero now removes it.** On the wishlist, the minus control no longer stops at one. Going below one takes the card off the wishlist.

### New

- **Decks:** Deck settings has an Empty a list section that clears the live list, or the theory list when theory is on, in one press (#340)
- **Collection:** in card view, cards in a normal folder have plus and minus controls for their copies, while decks and Recently removed do not (#337)
- **Wishlist:** card view has a plus and minus control for each wish, beside the pencil (#337)
- **General:** in folder views of decks, the wishlist and the collection, a tile at the top takes a card or folder up one level (#338)

### Changed

- **Decks:** a new deck made from inside a folder is filed in that folder by default, and the form can still choose another folder (#339)
- **Wishlist:** stepping a wish down from one now goes to zero and removes the card from the wishlist, where it used to stop at one (#337)

### Fixed

- **Decks:** switching the theory list off no longer blanks the window (#330)
- **Decks:** the clear confirmations read correctly for one card instead of saying the 1 card in it leave the deck (#340)
- **Decks:** a question about clearing the theory list no longer stays open after the theory list is switched off (#340)
- **General:** dragging by a card's picture works again after opening a deck editor, instead of needing a reload (#341)
