### Highlights

- **Clicking a printing in a deck now changes the deck to that printing.** From a deck row you can no longer browse a printing without committing to it, so look at printings in the card pane instead.
- **The deck stats moved below the deck and their toggle is gone.** The four charts now sit in a band under the deck rather than in a side panel you could open and close.
- **Enabling the theory list moves your deck into it.** The deck you built becomes the theory list, and your live list starts empty and fills as you acquire cards.
- **Search hides printings that no format allows.** Art cards, tokens and playtest cards are hidden by default, and an Unplayable chip beside All printings brings them back.
- **Cards are now filed into deck piles by what they do.** Piles such as Removal, Ramp and Draw are chosen from the tags that describe each card, with its card type used when no tag fits.

### New

- **Decks:** you can set up a whole deck when you create it, including description, notes, cover art, folder and theory, and the cover picker searches every printing (#50)
- **Decks:** the quick add field suggests up to five matching cards as you type, and Enter or a click adds the one you pick (#44)
- **Decks:** a split X option under mana value grouping puts cards with X in their cost into their own group, placed after 8 or more and before unknown (#43)
- **Decks:** each deck remembers the tab, grouping and sort you last used and reopens there (#45)
- **Search:** a gold Game Changer crown appears in the top right of results for cards the Commander bracket counts (#38)
- **Search:** an X chip in the mana value filters finds cards with X in their cost, and picking it together with a number still finds each card once (#43)
- **Collection:** the mana value filter has an X chip that finds cards with X in their cost (#43)
- **General:** hold ctrl and scroll over a card section to resize its cards, with a small zoom badge that fades after a moment (#39)
- **General:** the printings list can group by artist, release date or set, or show a flat list sorted by price, and it remembers your choice (#42)
- **General:** a button shows the foil sheen over a printing's art when that printing comes in both nonfoil and foil or etched (#42)

### Changed

- **Search:** search shows each card once, as the cheapest printing of its most recent release at the shop you pick, rather than its newest printing (#41)
- **Search:** art cards, tokens, emblems, memorabilia and playtest cards are hidden by default, and an Unplayable chip beside All printings brings them back (#36)
- **Search:** the format list and set picker are in alphabetical order, with options that no result can use moved to the bottom (#40)
- **Collection:** the format and sort choices are in alphabetical order (#40)
- **Wishlist:** the sort choices are in alphabetical order (#40)
- **General:** folder names are sorted alphabetically with numbers in order, so Cube 2 comes before Cube 10 (#40)
- **Decks:** the deck stacks no longer draw an outline around a pile at rest, and a switched-off pile shows only by its faded look (#46)
- **Decks:** the deck stacks wrap their piles downward instead of scrolling sideways when a deck has many categories (#48)
- **Decks:** the Sideboard stays on the right edge while the other piles scroll beneath it, and stays there even when it is empty (#46, #48)
- **Decks:** in the deck stacks each card's price and printing sit on a strip beneath its picture, its copy count shows on its tag, and a Game Changer banner sits in its corner (#34)
- **Decks:** the controls on a deck stack card appear when the card is opened, not when the pointer passes over it (#34)
- **Decks:** a pile flips through more slowly, so the eye can follow each card as it moves (#37)
- **Decks:** the deck stats are a band below the deck instead of a side panel, and the Stats button is gone (#49)
- **Decks:** turning on the theory list moves the deck's cards into it and leaves the live list empty, and the tabs now read Theory then Live (#45)
- **Decks:** a duplicated deck opens on its default view instead of the view the original was left on (#45)
- **Decks:** clicking a printing in a deck swaps the deck to that printing, and the printing the deck already uses is marked In deck (#42)
- **Decks:** a deck's piles are filed by the tags that describe what each card does, with card type used when no tag fits, and lands always go to Land (#47)
- **Decks:** an empty pile no longer shows a heading, except the four standard piles of Commander, Sideboard, Companion and Maybeboard (#47)
- **Decks:** the deck mana curve can show the split X group as a tenth bar, while its average mana value stays the same (#43)
- **Decks:** the deck format, Group by and Sort choices are in alphabetical order (#40)

### Fixed

- **Decks:** screen readers now read each deck card's price and printing, which they could not before (#34)
- **Search:** the marks on card art can now show their explanations when hovered, which did not work before (#38)
