### Highlights

- **The Android app can no longer connect a membership.** Sync on the Android app now starts by pairing with a device that already syncs. The desktop app and the web app still connect memberships as before.
- **The scanner now works in the web app and on phones.** The phone's Scanner tab and the web app can recognise cards with the camera. The first time the scanner is used it offers to download about 19 MB of card data.
- **Several sync problems are fixed.** Cards that were dropped or put in the wrong folder when devices synced are now kept and placed correctly.

### New

- **Scanner:** the phone's Scanner tab now recognises cards with the camera, shows a tray of the cards it has found, and adds them to a folder you choose (#858)
- **Scanner:** the first time the scanner is used on an install without its card data it offers to download about 19 MB, with a progress bar, and it works straight away afterwards (#859)
- **Web:** the web app can scan cards with the camera, and cards found are added to the collection the same way as in the desktop app (#861)
- **Settings:** the privacy policy can be opened from Settings, and it is published on the website and stays readable offline after an update (#862)

### Changed

- **Android:** the Android app no longer offers to connect a membership or shows one on the Sync screen, so a phone joins sync by pairing with a device that already syncs (#860)
- **Android:** the Android app has its own app icon in place of the default one (#862)

### Fixed

- **Sync:** a new phone or browser paired with a desktop that has not updated no longer loses some of its cards on first sync (#847)
- **Sync:** a card moved or changed on one device is no longer dropped with an error when another device has already added the same card and not yet synced it, and the two copies now merge into one (#854, #856)
- **Sync:** cards filed in a folder that another device had deleted are put back in the right place when that folder returns, instead of going to the root or being deleted (#855)
- **Scanner:** on the web app at desktop widths and on Android tablets the scanner no longer starts its camera only to be refused on every frame (#852)
- **Sharing:** the Share button no longer appears in the web app or on Android tablets, where folders cannot be shared, so it no longer ends in an error (#849)
