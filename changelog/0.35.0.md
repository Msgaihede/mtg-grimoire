### Highlights

- **Decks now have a to-do list.** Each deck gets a checklist below its notes, and a new To-dos widget on the home page gathers open to-dos from all your decks.
- **Game helpers now appear on deck tokens.** A deck's token section adds The Monarch, The Ring, The Initiative, City's Blessing, Day and Night and dungeons when its cards bring them.

### New

- **Decks:** a to-do list band sits below a deck's notes where you can type a checklist, nest items with Tab, tick them off and delete them, and it saves on its own (#681)
- **General:** a to-do widget on the home page lists open to-dos across your decks, lets you tick them off in place, and opens the deck when you press its name, with settings for which decks it shows, their order and whether completed items appear (#681)
- **Decks:** a deck's token section shows the game helpers its cards bring, such as The Monarch, The Ring, The Initiative, City's Blessing, Day and Night, Start Your Engines! and dungeons (#680)
- **Decks:** the All tokens list now includes dungeons (#680)
- **General:** a filter bar stays at the top of card search, the collection, the wishlist and Tags as you scroll down the grid, with search, colours, mana value, filters, sort and a reset button (#683)

### Changed

- **Decks:** a token with zero copies in a deck's tokens section shows its card art faded, while its counter and details stay at full strength (#677)
- **Decks:** when a card that makes a token is cut from a deck, a token you still have copies of stays in the deck with a red outline and a not made by deck badge instead of being deleted, and undo brings the card and its token back together (#682)

### Fixed

- **Wishlist:** under Missing, a deck's managed wishlist no longer asks you to buy a token again when you already hold it in another art or finish, while the other modes still compare exact printing and finish (#679)
