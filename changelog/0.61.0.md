### Highlights

- **The scanner has one mode now.** The Fast and Exact modes and their settings are gone, so every scan uses the Neural scanner, and the scanner no longer offers to download extra recognition data.
- **The scanner page has a new layout.** The picture, the scanned-card tray and the controls are rearranged on the desktop app and on phones, and a card added at a higher price is celebrated with confetti.

### New

- **Decks:** you can set how loud the playtest board's sounds are with a volume slider that appears under the speaker button when you hover over it or reach it with the keyboard, and each device keeps its own level (#1392)
- **Scanner:** a burst of confetti appears over the picture when a card is added, with more pieces and colours for higher prices and a rain and gold flash for the highest prices, and none when reduced motion is on (#1401)

### Changed

- **Scanner:** the scanner has only the Neural mode, so the mode picker, the speed and accuracy sliders and the offer to download extra data are gone (#1391)
- **Scanner:** when a card's printing is made in only one finish, the scanner uses that finish, and otherwise the finish is shown as unknown (#1391)
- **Scanner:** on the desktop the scan controls sit in a bar under the picture, the Defaults popover is gone and the condition setting moved to the footer of the tray (#1401)
- **Scanner:** the scanned-card tray shows the total worth and a gold button that jumps to the next card needing a decision, and each card is one tile with its finish, quantity, name and price (#1401)
- **Scanner:** on a phone the folder and add row sits at the top, the tray is a strip you tap to open, point and shoot works by holding the picture, and sideways the picture and tray sit in two columns (#1401)
- **Decks:** the playtesting setup page has the same title bar as the other pages, with a trail back to all decks in place of the folder path, and less space at the top (#1397)
