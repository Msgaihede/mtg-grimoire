### Highlights

- **The card size slider is gone from the draft pick screen.** Ctrl and the mouse wheel over the draft still resize the cards, and the zoom you saved still applies. Use either of those instead of the slider.
- **Unsaved draft decks stay out of your decks until you save them.** A deck you build in a draft no longer appears in your decks, search, deck pickers or on your other devices until you save it from the draft page. Drafts made before this update become ordinary decks.
- **Each pick can go to your deck or your sideboard, and the deck is the default.** A double-click or Pick puts a card in your deck, while the sideboard button or Shift and Enter puts it in the sideboard. Before, every pick went to the pool and you moved cards into the deck afterwards.

### New

- **General:** you can pick a card on the draft pick screen by double-clicking it, and a triple-click picks it only once (#1480)
- **General:** each picked card can go to your deck or your sideboard, you can move a picked card between the two by clicking it, and at build time the deck's cards are sorted into categories while sideboard cards go to the draft deck's pool (#1488)
- **General:** you can show the draft pool as a compact tray beside the pack instead of stacked columns under it, and the choice is remembered on this device (#1489)
- **General:** resting the pointer on a card in the pack, in a face-up pile or in the pool shows that card large beside the pointer (#1486)
- **General:** after leaving the draft page or reloading the window, you can rejoin a pod draft from the Draft in progress list (#1487)
- **Prices:** you can price opened packs at TCGplayer and Cardmarket together, and when they are in different currencies the other currency is converted to your app currency at the European Central Bank's daily rate instead of replacing your first pick (#1476)
- **General:** the product list on an Open Packs set page has a search box that finds products by name, by kind or by type such as collector box or sealed (#1484)
- **General:** an Open Packs set page has a searchable Set picker that lets you switch to another set without going back to the set list (#1485)

### Changed

- **General:** the card size slider is gone from the draft pick screen, and ctrl with the mouse wheel over the draft is the only way to resize the cards (#1479)
- **General:** the opened cards in Open Packs look like the cards in the other grids, with the set, collector number, finish and price shown under each card (#1475)
- **Prices:** the price controls in Open Packs sit in a column on the right beside the product row, and the expected value is shown there before a pack is opened (#1483)
- **Decks:** a deck you build in a draft stays out of your decks, search, deck pickers and other devices until you save it from the draft page, and drafts made before this update become ordinary decks (#1490)

### Fixed

- **General:** the drop area for the pool on the pick screen stays the same height when the pool is empty and after the first pick (#1478)
- **Decks:** a deck made after a draft deck was deleted is no longer treated as a draft, and Discard can no longer delete a deck that is not a draft (#1481)
