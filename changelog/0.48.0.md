### Highlights

- **Playtest a deck solo with the new goldfish board.** A goldfish button in the deck editor opens a solo playtest of the deck against an opponent who does nothing, on the desktop app only. Attacks, answers to target choices and an AI opponent are not built yet.

### New

- **Decks:** a goldfish button in the deck editor opens a solo playtest of the deck, available on the desktop app only (#956)
- **Decks:** a playtest starts with a mulligan step where you choose which cards go to the bottom of the library, with a free first mulligan for commander decks (#956)
- **Decks:** the playtest table shows creatures in front, lands on the left and other permanents on the right, identical cards stacked with a count, tapped cards turned sideways and counters as badges (#956)
- **Decks:** you can drag cards between the hand, the table, the library, the graveyard, exile and the command zone, click to tap, and double-click to play a card (#956)
- **Decks:** right-click menus during a playtest offer counters, token copies, drawing, scrying, surveilling, milling, searching, shuffling and untapping everything, and a next turn button moves the game on (#956)
- **Decks:** casts are paid from untapped mana with the commander tax applied, and a play the engine cannot complete is placed unpaid and recorded in the game log (#956)
- **Decks:** a hover preview, a help panel and a game log sit beside the playtest table, and the playtest never ends in a loss even at 0 life (#956)

### Fixed

- **Decks:** adding several picked cards to a deck from the right-click menu now adds every one of them instead of only the last (#958)
- **Decks:** putting a label on several selected cards from a deck's right-click menu now labels all of them instead of only the one clicked (#958)
- **Wishlist:** right-click menu actions on several picked wishes now apply to all of them instead of only the one clicked, and wishes with no printing are left out (#958)
- **Collection:** cards without a price now sit at the bottom of a price-sorted collection in name order instead of looking shuffled (#959)
- **Wishlist:** cards without a price now sit at the bottom of a price-sorted wishlist in name order instead of looking shuffled (#959)
- **Sharing:** cards without a price now sit at the bottom of a price-sorted shared binder page in name order instead of looking shuffled (#959)
