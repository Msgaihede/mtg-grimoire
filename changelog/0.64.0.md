### Highlights

- **Your AI agent can read your data but no longer add cards.** An AI agent on this computer can look up cards and read your collection, wishlist and decks, but it can no longer add cards to your collection, wishlist or a deck. You turn the connection on in Settings, then Integrations.
- **Build your own home widgets.** Customize has a Custom widgets dialog where you preview each custom widget and add it to the page. An AI agent can write, check, change and delete these widgets for you.
- **Trade online with another reader.** On the desktop app, two readers can trade over a six-character lobby code. Each keeps their own copy of the trade, and both finish it on their own collection and wishlist.
- **Pick cards by dragging them onto your pool.** In the draft, drag a card from the pack onto your pool to pick it. Cards in the pack are now sorted by rarity by default.

### New

- **Settings:** turn on AI agents on this computer in Settings and an agent such as Claude Desktop, Claude Code or Cursor can search cards and read your collection, wishlist and decks, with nothing leaving your computer and no membership needed (#1423)
- **Settings:** an AI agent can ask detailed read-only questions across all of your data (#1427)
- **Settings:** an AI agent connected to the app gets three built-in guides, for searching cards, creating a home widget and exploring your data (#1444)
- **Settings:** Settings, then Integrations shows set-up steps for Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI, Antigravity and Cursor, and Claude Desktop can install the connection in one click (#1441)
- **Settings:** Settings, then Integrations has a review list where you apply or decline changes that integrations ask to make to your collection or wishlist, with switches for applying them automatically, ready for integrations that are coming such as Discord (#1422)
- **Sharing:** two readers on the desktop app can trade online, with each offer shown on both screens and both sides accepting the same offer before the trade is agreed (#1426)
- **Sync:** when the app opens after being closed for a while, the status line says it is syncing with your other devices and the loading bar moves until your latest changes have arrived (#1436)
- **General:** Customize has a Custom widgets dialog where you preview each custom widget and add it to the page (#1430)
- **General:** an AI agent can preview, check, change and delete the custom home widgets it made (#1442)
- **General:** a custom widget can have settings that change what data it asks for, such as an Archived decks setting on the starter Deck shelf, and the card keeps showing while the new data loads (#1443)

### Changed

- **Settings:** AI agents can no longer add cards to your collection, wishlist or decks, and their only writes are to custom home widgets (#1444)
- **General:** in the draft, dragging a card from the pack onto your pool picks it, and a double-click on a card no longer picks it, so you choose it and press Pick or Enter (#1435)
- **General:** the draft pack is sorted by rarity by default, with mythic first and common last, and a Sort menu lets you choose As dealt instead (#1438)
- **General:** the set lists in the draft and Open Packs now show only sets that have booster packs, so bonus sheets, commander and promo sets no longer appear, and the lists refresh when card data updates (#1437)
- **General:** a custom widget's settings are in the card's settings popover, each change takes effect as you make it, and the Save button is gone (#1440)

### Fixed

- **Decks:** a card in hand that has cycling can be activated from its menu during playtesting, instead of only being playable (#1439)
- **General:** holding Control and scrolling over the draft or the opened packs zooms the cards instead of zooming the whole window, and the pack's Card size slider shares that zoom (#1445)
