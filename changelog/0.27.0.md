### Highlights

- **The scanner gets two modes and a review tray.** Fast and Exact modes, set and release-date filters, and scanned cards that wait in a review tray until you add them to your collection. The older scanner panels now sit behind a Developer switch.
- **Deleting a wishlist folder can take its wishes with it.** A new option deletes a folder together with every wish inside it and its sub-folders. Clearing a folder removes only the wishes filed directly in it and keeps the folder.
- **Text in the deck editor can no longer be dragged.** Dragging across the deck editor no longer selects its headings and cards, and dialogs and panels still let you select text.
- **You can open more than one window onto your collection.** Two decks can sit side by side, and changes made in one window show up in the other within about a quarter of a second.

### New

- **Scanner:** the scanner has a Fast mode that recognises a card quickly from its picture and an Exact mode that checks several frames and, when unsure, offers up to 12 printings to pick from (#469)
- **Scanner:** you can limit the scanner to chosen sets or a range of release dates, and an impossible filter is refused with a message saying why (#469)
- **Scanner:** scanned cards wait in a review tray, a card scanned again moves up its count, and adding the tray to the collection adds every card in it and empties the tray (#469)
- **Scanner:** the card recognition data now comes built into the app (#469)
- **Wishlist:** you can clear a folder, which removes the wishes filed directly in it and keeps the folder and its sub-folders (#476)
- **Wishlist:** you can delete a folder together with every wish in it and in its sub-folders, while the plain delete still moves its wishes to the top level (#476)
- **General:** you can open more than one window onto the same collection, using Ctrl+Shift+N for a new window (#478)
- **General:** changes made in one window, such as deck edits, collection adds and marketplace changes, appear in the other windows within about a quarter of a second, while each window keeps its own view settings (#478)

### Changed

- **Scanner:** the older scanner panels are now hidden behind a Developer switch (#469)
- **Scanner:** only one window can scan at a time, and a second window says so and does not open the camera (#478)
- **General:** opening the app again while it is running opens a new window instead of doing nothing visible, and the app closes only when its last window closes (#478)
- **Decks:** text in the deck editor can no longer be selected by dragging, while text in dialogs, the format check, the validation panel, the deck bracket panel and the notes still can be selected (#477)

### Fixed

- **Decks:** dragging across the deck editor no longer selects a large block of headings and card frames, and a selected piece of text can no longer be dragged out of the page (#477)
