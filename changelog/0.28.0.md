### Highlights

- **Deck notes have a new look.** Notes in a deck are now cards in a grid that grow to fit their text. Writing, editing and naming cards now happens in dialogs rather than by opening a row in place.
- **Sticky notes, a printings widget and zoom on the home page.** The home page gains a sticky notes widget and a new printings widget, and the whole dashboard can now be zoomed with ctrl and scroll.
- **Removing a card no longer strands you.** After removing the card you are looking at, the card window moves on to the next card instead of staying on the removed one.

### New

- **Decks:** the deck header's Lands figure shows a dim count of modal double-faced cards that can be played as a land off the back, such as Lands 32 +5 MDFC (#482)
- **General:** the home page can be zoomed with ctrl and scroll in the same steps as the card grids, and the zoom is remembered (#486)
- **General:** a sticky notes widget for the home page with a board you can reorder, and a New note button that opens the editor straight away (#490)
- **General:** a new printings widget on the home page lists recent reprints of cards in the decks you watch, grouped by release day, with a choice of languages and a list of the decks holding each card (#488)

### Changed

- **Decks:** deck notes are cards in a masonry grid instead of rows that unfold in place, and the card picker offers chips by card type (#492)
- **General:** the combos section in a card's window is now a list of combos on the left with the chosen one shown in full on the right, more combos load as you scroll instead of through a Show more button, the counts sit in one line above the list, and the bracket letter is no longer shown (#487)

### Fixed

- **General:** after removing a card from a deck, the collection or the wishlist while its window is open, the window moves to the next card or back to the previous one, undoing the removal returns you to that card, and the arrow buttons keep working (#484)
- **General:** dialogs in a narrow window sit with equal space on both sides instead of being pushed off to the right (#487)
