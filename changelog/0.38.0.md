### Highlights

- **The scanner has a new layout.** The camera now has a strip above it that shows what the scanner is doing, a menu for choosing your webcam, and a larger view of each card as it is added.

### New

- **Scanner:** you can choose which webcam the scanner uses from a camera menu in the scan bar, and the choice is remembered on that computer (#730)
- **Scanner:** each card the scanner adds is shown over the bottom of the camera for about two seconds, with its picture, a tick, its name, set, number and finish (#730)
- **Scanner:** the scanned cards can be shown as a grid as well as a list (#730)

### Changed

- **Scanner:** a strip above the camera now shows the scanner's current state, the card it is matching or has just added, a short sentence and a progress bar, replacing the status line under the camera (#730)
- **Scanner:** on a wide window the camera takes most of the space beside the scanned card list, and on a narrower window the card list is wider than before (#730)
- **Scanner:** the camera picture now fills its box and is cropped to fit instead of being shown with empty borders (#730)
