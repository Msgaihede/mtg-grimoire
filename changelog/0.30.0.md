### Highlights

- **Deck wishlists now keep themselves up to date.** A Theory + Actual deck has its own wishlist folder, named after the deck, that lists the cards its compare list shows as missing. The folder is read-only and appears under Managed by decks. A Managed wishlist switch in Deck settings controls it and is on by default.
- **Recently removed can now be cleared.** A Clear button empties the Recently removed folder in one step after a confirmation. Its copies can now also be changed and removed from the collection.
- **Price history for each mover.** Press a row in the Price movers widget on the home page to see that copy's price history over 7 days, 30 days or all time.
- **Quick add now asks which wishlist copy to use.** When you quick add copies to a deck and remove them from the wishlist, a popup always lets you choose which wishlist line the copies come off.

### New

- **Decks:** the deck editor's Group by option has Matches theory on the Actual tab of a Theory + Actual deck, sorting cards into Exact Match, Art Mismatch and No Match (#518)
- **Decks:** deck notes can be reordered by dragging their grip or with the arrow keys (#521)
- **Decks:** the Sideboard, Maybeboard and switched-off piles in the Stacks view's right-hand rail can be reordered by dragging their grip or with the arrow keys, within their own part of the rail (#520)
- **Decks:** a new Deck settings option shows the deck's tokens and emblems as a pile in the Stacks, Grid, Text and Table views, off by default, and tokens never count toward the deck (#523)
- **Decks:** a Hide button on the Mana pips card in deck stats leaves colorless out of the Cost and Sources bars (#525)
- **Collection:** copies in Recently removed can be changed with quantity steppers and removed from the collection from the right-click menu, and the folder can be cleared all at once (#522)
- **Search:** pressing a set's name in the card details popup closes it and opens Search showing only that set, with the format set to Any card (#519)
- **Wishlist:** a Theory + Actual deck keeps a wishlist folder named after it, holding the cards its compare list shows as missing, controlled by a Managed wishlist switch in Deck settings that is on by default (#526)
- **Prices:** each row of the Price movers widget opens a price history popup for that copy, with the card's picture, a price graph and the change, low and high over 7 days, 30 days or all time (#529)

### Changed

- **Decks:** the deck card's right-click menu is regrouped, with Collection link moved under Add to and Move to renamed Category (#517)
- **Decks:** quick add and remove from wishlist always asks which wishlist copy to take from, listing every wish for that card in any printing or finish, not only the one in the deck (#527)
- **Search:** the Exact option for colour filters is always shown in the filter panel as its own toggle, instead of appearing only after a colour is picked, and picking or clearing colours no longer switches it off (#499)
- **Collection:** right-clicking a card and choosing Add to then Collection adds it at once in the finish on screen, without a finish picker, though a folder picker still appears when you have collection folders (#516)
- **General:** pressing a row in New printings opens the card drawn large beside the watched decks that hold it, in place of the small popup that listed only the decks (#528)

### Fixed

- **Decks:** with several deck cards selected, the Collection link menu counts and acts on all of them instead of only the card you right-clicked (#524)
- **General:** a reprint in the New printings list now shows the watched decks that hold it, and its picture now appears on the web and Android apps (#528)
