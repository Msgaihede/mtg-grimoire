### Highlights

- **Life tracker on Android and the web.** The Android app and the web app have a new Life tab for tracking life totals in games of up to six players, with commander damage, poison, a turn timer and saved games.
- **Deck colors on the Home page.** A new Deck colors widget files your decks under every colour combination in Magic, so you can see which colours you have decks in and which have none.
- **Export formats moved behind More formats.** MTGO, Moxfield and TCGplayer are no longer among the four buttons in the export dialog; you now find them under More formats.
- **A volume slider for the app's sounds.** Settings has a new Sound section where you can set how loud the app's sounds are on this device.

### New

- **Decks:** a readout beside the deck editor's filter shows how many cards on screen count toward deck size, plus the sideboard and maybeboard cards that do not count (#1711)
- **General:** the Home page has a Deck colors widget that shows every colour combination with your decks filed under it, and empty slots for combinations without a deck (#1710)
- **Import & export:** the export dialog shows a few example lines in the chosen format under the options, so you can see what the file will look like before you export (#1705)
- **Settings:** settings has a new Sound section with an app volume slider and a Play button that plays a test sound at that level, applying to scanner sounds and the playtest board (#1709)
- **General:** the Life tab tracks life totals for games of one to six players, with formats, dead players, a calculator, commander damage, poison, monarch, a turn timer and a roulette for picking who goes first (#1712)
- **General:** on a phone, Back closes open panels in the life tracker first and then asks whether to end the game (#1712)
- **General:** a game in the life tracker is saved on the device, so a reload or closing the app keeps it, and the setup offers Continue game (#1712)
- **Android:** in the Android app the status and navigation bars hide while you play the life tracker, and a swipe from the edge shows them briefly (#1712)

### Changed

- **Import & export:** the export dialog's format choices show Plain text, Arena, Archidekt and CSV as buttons, with MTGO, Moxfield and TCGplayer under a More formats dropdown that has a search box; on a phone the four buttons now fit on one line (#1704)
- **General:** on Windows the app's sound shows in the volume mixer as MTG Grimoire with the app's icon instead of Microsoft Edge WebView2 (#1709)
- **Settings:** searching for volume now leads to the new Sound panel, and the word no longer appears under scan sounds (#1709)
