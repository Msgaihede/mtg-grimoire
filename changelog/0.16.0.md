### Highlights

- **A plain-text copy of your data.** Your decks, collection and wishlist can now be kept as a plain-text copy that updates as you change them. You set it up from a new panel in Settings.

### New

- **Settings:** a new panel in Settings for keeping a plain-text copy of your decks, collection and wishlist (#233)
- **Backup:** the plain-text copy of your decks, collection and wishlist updates by itself when you change them (#233)

### Fixed

- **Search:** searching no longer stalls while the plain-text copy is being updated (#233)
