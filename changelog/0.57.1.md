### Highlights

- **Removing a device or leaving a group can now be refused.** If this device has not yet heard of every device in the group, removing one or leaving is refused and you are told to do it from a device that has the full list.
- **Typed quantities apply on Enter or when you leave the box.** A number typed into a quantity box is no longer applied keystroke by keystroke, so half-typed numbers can no longer cut or delete copies. Escape throws the typed number away.
- **Quick add from the collection refuses extra copies.** Adding a copy to a deck from the collection is refused when the deck does not need that many at that printing or finish.
- **Automatic backups are thinned out.** Only the newest three automatic full backups of each kind are kept, so older ones are removed as new ones are made.

### New

- **Android:** on a phone on mobile data, the card data download can be offered again when you return to the app, and a Download card data button appears in search and settings while it is waiting (#1274)

### Changed

- **Backup:** only the newest three automatic full backups of each kind are kept, and older ones are removed (#1260)
- **Sync:** a paired device with no subscription shows Off instead of Offline and no longer records errors while it waits for one (#1270)
- **Sync:** removing a device or leaving a group is refused when this device has not heard of every device in the group, and you are asked to do it from a device that has the full list (#1290)
- **Sharing:** shared binder and deck pictures load only as you scroll to them (#1254)
- **Sharing:** shared pages show card pictures only from Scryfall and replace any other picture with a card frame (#1275)
- **Scanner:** frames sent to the scanner that are too large or not JPEG are refused rather than read (#1309)
- **Decks:** adding a copy to a deck from the collection is refused when the deck does not need that many at that printing or finish (#1316)
- **General:** typing a number into a quantity box applies it when you press Enter or leave the box, and Escape discards it (#1291)
