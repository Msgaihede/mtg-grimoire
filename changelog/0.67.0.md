### Highlights

- **Open Packs now counts cards under your minimum at the reduced price.** The pack and session totals, the expected value and the chances all use the reduced price, so cards you will not sell count for less. Hover over a total to see how it is worked out.
- **A pack opener mode for the scanner.** The camera fills the window, with the controls in a bar along the bottom and the results in a drawer on the right. The bar fades out after three seconds without any input.
- **A bulk price for your collection's value.** The collection value setting has a bulk price for cards under the minimum, and every collection value figure counts them at that price.

### New

- **Prices:** open packs can price cards under the minimum at a bulk price you set, so they add a small amount instead of nothing (#1492)
- **Settings:** the collection value setting has a bulk price for cards under the minimum, used in the collection bar, folder totals, home widgets and value graph (#1505)
- **Scanner:** a pack opener mode makes the camera fill the window, with the controls in a bar along the bottom, results in a drawer on the right and a light in the corner showing whether it is scanning, stopped or ready (#1513)
- **General:** a reset button in Open Packs starts the session's running tally over at once without restarting the app (#1509)
- **General:** pressing the sidebar entry of the page you are already on, or its keyboard shortcut, takes you back to that page's top level (#1515)
- **General:** on a wide window the draft pick screen shows deck stats with a mana curve, cards by type and a colour pie, counting only the cards you have picked for your deck (#1516)

### Changed

- **Prices:** open packs' totals, expected value, simulation, histogram and chance to beat what you paid all use the reduced price, and cards under the minimum show their price in red (#1512)
- **General:** in Open Packs, picking a product from the list opens it straight away, so the separate open press is no longer needed, and picking the product already shown does nothing (#1514)
- **General:** the set tiles in Open Packs and draft pack order are larger, with the set symbol filling most of each tile and the set code moved to the top right (#1508)
- **General:** on the draft pick screen the deck and sideboard sit side by side under the pack, the sideboard's curve is grouped into four columns headed by mana symbols, and the rounds and the table share one row when they fit (#1516)
- **General:** draft side tray rows show each card's mana cost before its name (#1516)
- **Scanner:** the minus button on a scanner tray row removes the card when it is down to one copy, and typing zero removes it too (#1510)

### Fixed

- **Collection:** dragging a set of picked cards or wishes onto a folder files all of them, not only the one you grabbed (#1506)
- **Decks:** every card taken out of a deck, by swapping its printing, changing its finish, splitting it, lowering its quantity or undoing an add, now sends its copies to Recently removed so no leftover copy stays in the deck's folder (#1511)
