### Highlights

- **Playtesting can now take control of cards.** The sandbox can move a permanent to or from an opponent's side, and you can cast an opponent's card when the rules allow it.
- **The Life tab holds still while a game runs.** On a phone, the board keeps the way it was facing when the game started and the screen stays on until the game ends or you leave the tab.

### New

- **General:** on a phone, while a Life tracker game is on screen the board keeps the way it was facing when the game started and the screen stays on instead of dimming or turning with the phone (#1717)
- **General:** in playtesting, a permanent can be given to an opponent or taken from them, and a card from an opponent's graveyard or exile can be put onto the battlefield under your control, with the log saying what happened (#1721)

### Changed

- **General:** in the life tracker, the calculator opened from another player's commander damage panel now works for the player who is counting the damage rather than for the commander's own seat (#1718)
- **General:** an opponent's graveyard or exile dialog in playtesting says Their battlefield, and on a permanent card it offers Your battlefield (#1721)

### Fixed

- **General:** on the life tracker, the commander damage panels of the other players now face the player counting the damage, so their minus and plus read the right way up for that player (#1718)
- **General:** in playtesting, a card in your graveyard or exile with an ability you can use, such as Unearth, now shows an Activate option in its menu and in the look-through dialog (#1720)
- **General:** in playtesting, a card in an opponent's graveyard or exile that the rules let you cast can now be cast, as with Hostage Taker's hostage (#1721)
