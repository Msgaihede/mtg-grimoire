### Highlights

- **Deck labels stay put.** A label you put on a deck card now survives changing its printing, moving the card, and merging copies of it.
- **Sync is sturdier.** Several sync problems are fixed, including a device that changes group and pulls that left some screens showing old data.
- **Tighter security on the desktop app.** The camera only works from the app's own pages, shared links are checked more strictly, and updates must carry a valid signature to be installed.
- **Quick-add shows more.** Quick-add now lists ten suggestions with a count of the rest, and its plus buttons stand out on card art.

### New

- **Sync:** the Sync panel shows Membership ended when a group's membership has ended for this device (#660)

### Changed

- **Decks:** the plus on each tile in the deck editor's search column has a dark backing so it is easy to see on card art (#650)
- **Decks:** the toolbar that appears when you scroll down now docks flush under the header, and on wide windows its buttons show their words (#652)
- **Decks:** quick-add shows ten suggestions instead of five, with a line counting the rest such as +23 more (#655)
- **Decks:** the deck type caption explains that theory is the list you plan to build and actual is the cards you have sleeved up, and some deck wording is shorter (#669)
- **Search:** the plus on tiles in search results, the Tags page, and the collection and wishlist search sidebars has a dark backing so it shows on card art (#656)
- **Wishlist:** wishlist wording counts cards and items instead of wishes, and an empty folder reads No cards in this folder yet (#664)
- **Settings:** settings wording is clearer, for example the Start page option replaces Opening view and progress messages say Loading (#666)
- **Import & export:** import and export messages read Couldn't instead of Could not, and the export option that was called Fields is now Label color (#667)
- **General:** wording across the home page and the rest of the app is plainer, with Couldn't in place of Could not and US spellings such as color (#661, #668)
- **General:** the image cache removes images not used for 90 days and keeps itself under 512 MB (#651)
- **General:** optional price and tag data waits until the card download finishes on a first run (#651)
- **General:** a data file that cannot be used is not downloaded again on every launch and waits a day before it is tried again (#651)
- **General:** the desktop app refuses an update unless its download carries a valid signature (#653)
- **Scanner:** the camera only works for the app's own pages, so other pages in the window cannot use it (#653)
- **Sharing:** shared deck links are read only over secure connections, are size-limited, and must come from the page's own site (#653)
- **Sync:** changes stamped more than a day ahead of this device's clock are held back, with a note on the Sync panel, until the clock is corrected (#660)
- **Sync:** very large changes are sent in smaller pieces, and each group is capped at 128 MB of stored changes (#660)

### Fixed

- **Decks:** a deck card keeps its label when its printing is changed (#647)
- **Decks:** a deck card keeps its label when it is moved onto a copy of itself, when a category is deleted, or when a printing update merges it with another copy (#658)
- **Decks:** a card merged after a printing update lands on the copies of its own finish rather than the other finish (#662)
- **Search:** the unpressed color identity chip reads Within instead of Exact (#668)
- **Prices:** mirrored prices are kept up to date even when a sync fails partway through (#651)
- **Backup:** two installations pointed at the same mirror folder no longer delete each other's decks, and the second one is refused (#651)
- **Sync:** after a pull, sticky notes, muted tags and a card's holdings refresh at once, so an edit is not made on stale data (#660)
- **Sync:** a device unheard from for 90 days no longer holds back cleanup for the rest of its group (#660)
- **Sync:** pressing Leave group during a slow sync waits its turn instead of failing with a busy message (#660)
- **Sync:** a device that moves to another group starts that group's history from the beginning (#665)
- **General:** old update downloads are cleaned up at launch (#651)
- **General:** an interrupted card download is started again from the beginning rather than joined to a different file (#651)
