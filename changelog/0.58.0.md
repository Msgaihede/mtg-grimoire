### Highlights

- **Playtest games now stay in progress when you leave the page.** A game carries on when you go to another page and come back. Ending it, or starting a new game, now asks you to confirm first while a game is underway.
- **The scanner now opens stopped.** The camera comes on when you open the Scanner, but no card is read until you press Start scanning.
- **The scanner no longer matches tokens and emblems.** A token that shares a card's name or art can no longer be read in place of the real card. There is no switch to turn this back on.

### New

- **Scanner:** the scanned-card popup shows the card's price, or the price range when several printings match (#1345)
- **Scanner:** you can create a new collection folder from the folder picker in the scanner's tray (#1360)
- **Decks:** emblems you get during a playtest game are shown on the table, and their text appears when you hover them (#1353)
- **Decks:** during a playtest with commanders, the damage each commander has dealt to a player is shown on that player's plate, and reaching 21 is marked in red (#1355)
- **Decks:** during a playtest, each card shows small chips naming each effect that changes it and the card that causes it (#1349)
- **Decks:** during a playtest you can tap a land or other mana source for mana by clicking it, whether or not the sandbox is on, and the mana stays floating until you spend it (#1354)
- **Decks:** during a playtest you can drag cards to attack, block, play, equip or target, as well as clicking them (#1346)
- **Decks:** when the opponent's spell or ability is on the stack and you can respond, a large panel over their table shows it with a Let it resolve button (#1341)
- **Decks:** during a playtest you can rearrange your own hand at any time by dragging a card or pressing Shift with the left or right arrow key (#1347)
- **Decks:** a creature whose power or toughness has changed shows a corner chip with both numbers, green if raised and red if lowered (#1340)
- **Search:** card search has an Image updated sort that orders cards by when their picture was last changed, newest first by default (#1350)

### Changed

- **Scanner:** the Scanner opens stopped, so no card is read until you press Start scanning (#1351)
- **Scanner:** the scanner no longer matches tokens and emblems, so a token that shares a card's name or art cannot be read in place of the real card (#1337)
- **Scanner:** the scanned-cards list scrolls back to its top when a card is scanned, and on a phone the heading returns to view only if you had scrolled past it (#1339)
- **Scanner:** the scanner now reads the set symbol and collector number more often in Neural mode, so fewer cards are left for you to choose a printing (#1361)
- **Decks:** a playtest game is kept when you leave the playtest page and come back to it, and ending it or starting a new game asks you to confirm first while a game is underway (#1342)
- **Decks:** the playtest setup page is a game lobby, with your decks on the left and the table, with its seats, game mode and AI skill, on the right, and its text is shorter; the game mode tiles are now called Gamemode (#1356)
- **Decks:** each seat has a Theory or Actual switch for decks that keep both lists, and a tick or warning mark replaces the explanatory sentences (#1356)
- **Decks:** when an ability lets you cast a spell, the board first asks whether you want to cast it, and only then opens the card picker (#1348)
- **Decks:** the question panel shows the ability's own words beside the answers, so you can see what you are agreeing to (#1352)
- **Decks:** hovering an Aura or Equipment attached to a creature lifts it above its card so you can see it, and it drops back when the pointer leaves (#1343)
- **Search:** choosing Coming soon on a set opens that set sorted by Image updated, and unreleased cards show the date their image was last updated above their release date (#1350)

### Fixed

- **Scanner:** a card already lying under the camera is picked up when scanning starts, and is not added again after you close a dialog, restore the window or come back to the Scanner (#1338)
- **Decks:** the preview now opens for an Aura or Equipment drawn on another player's table, and you can tap your own Aura there with T (#1343)
- **Decks:** a planeswalker you put onto the table by hand now starts with its loyalty, so its abilities and emblems can be used (#1353)
