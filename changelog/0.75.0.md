### Highlights

- **Theory decks switch lists only when you press the chip.** The THEORY | ACTUAL chip on a theory deck is now a button, and the tile no longer switches lists on its own. Your choice is remembered for each deck.
- **Playtest games can be conceded.** Concede is in the game menu and asks you first. Effects you can end now appear as buttons beside Pass.
- **Playtest library order is hidden.** Outside the sandbox you can no longer see the order of your own library, and a card put on its top or bottom no longer gets its marker there.
- **Companions and sideboard cards in playtest.** Your companion can be revealed and put into your hand, and cards such as Living Wish can find cards from your sideboard.
- **Playtest shows more of the board.** The stack, exile links, storm count, turn order and player conditions are now shown during a playtest game.

### New

- **Decks:** the THEORY | ACTUAL chip on a theory deck's tile can be pressed to switch the card count, size warning, colour bar and sort between the two lists, even when the other list is empty and the list being shown is in bold (#1754)
- **General:** you can hold priority over your own spells and abilities, so the game stops for you to respond before they resolve, using the game menu row 'Hold priority on your spells' (#1757)
- **General:** the setup page has a 'Plays first' choice of coin flip, you or opponent (#1757)
- **General:** a search that finds one card for the battlefield and others for your hand, such as Cultivate, asks you which card goes onto the battlefield (#1757)
- **General:** the game log describes counters, taps, triggers, control changes, attachments, removals, energy, player counters, rooms, dungeons, votes, clashes and extra turns in plain sentences (#1762)
- **General:** your companion is revealed at the start of the game, and you can put it into your hand for three generic mana during your main phase when the stack is empty (#1761)
- **General:** cards such as Living Wish and Karn offer the cards from your sideboard and your companion to pick from, with each copy shown as its own card (#1761)
- **General:** you can concede a playtest game from the game menu, and it asks you to confirm first (#1758)
- **General:** effects you can end, such as Tempting Licid's effect, appear as buttons beside Pass with their mana cost shown (#1758)
- **General:** you can tap a mana source on the table by hand while paying for a spell or ability, and the payment stays open (#1759)
- **General:** cards on the stack show chips such as Copy or X=3, with their modes, paid costs and causes listed under the card's preview (#1763)
- **General:** a card's preview says which cards it exiled and which card it is exiled with, such as under Banishing Light (#1763)
- **General:** the middle line of the board shows the storm count when it is above zero (#1763)
- **General:** next-player chips after the turn tracker show the turn order, and a player who takes two turns in a row is marked as an extra turn (#1763)
- **General:** a player's plate shows red marks for conditions such as players can't gain life, next to the existing marks (#1763)

### Changed

- **Decks:** the THEORY ONLY label and its dashed border are gone, and the chip keeps the same width when its words change (#1754)
- **General:** when the game asks you to choose creatures to crew, counters to remove or move, triggers to order, or coin or die results to keep, you now answer each choice directly instead of from a short sample, with the engine's own short list behind 'Other answers' (#1756)
- **General:** blocker damage among several attackers is now split by you, instead of all going to the first attacker automatically (#1756)
- **General:** outside the sandbox you no longer see the order of your own library, and a card put on its top or bottom no longer gets its marker there (#1760)
- **General:** a source you must sacrifice to make mana, such as Lotus Petal, now asks you first before it is sacrificed (#1759)
- **General:** a card you exile face down without permission to look at it shows as a face-down card in the log instead of by its name (#1762)
