### Highlights

- **Search can show one row per card.** Turn on the one-row view to see each card once, with a count of its printings and the price range across them, and turn it off to see every printing as before.
- **The deck builder has been rebuilt.** Decks now sort cards into named categories shown as stacks, with four ways to view them, plus labels, folders, an optional Theory list, deck settings and a log of every change.
- **Foil and etched printings are marked on card art.** Cards that only exist as foil or etched show a shimmer and a small chip, so you can tell them apart at a glance.

### New

- **Decks:** a deck's cards can be sorted into named categories that you reorder and switch on or off, and a switched-off category counts toward nothing in the deck, with a count and total cost shown for each group (#18)
- **Decks:** a deck's cards show as stacks inside their groups, and you can view them as stacks, a table, plain text or a grid, grouped by category, mana value or type and sorted in several ways (#18)
- **Decks:** you can put coloured labels on cards in a deck, filter and sort by them, and get suggestions drawn from your other decks (#18)
- **Decks:** every change to a deck is kept in a log, grouped by date (#18)
- **Decks:** a deck can have an optional second list called Theory, with a list of differences from the live deck and a way to send cards to the wishlist (#18)
- **Decks:** decks can be sorted into folders, folders can sit inside other folders, and you can drag decks between them (#18)
- **Decks:** deck settings include a description, notes, and a cover chosen from one of the deck's card pictures or uploaded from your computer (#18)
- **Decks:** cards that break a format's rules and game changers are marked distinctly on the card (#18)
- **Decks:** deck list rows show a small glyph for foil-only and etched-only printings in place of the old letter
- **Search:** the search can show one row per card instead of one row per printing, with all printings one switch away, and the search panel in the deck editor does the same
- **Search:** a row for a card with several printings shows how many there are and the price range across them
- **Search:** sorting works in the one-row view as it does in the full list
- **Search:** filter options that would not change the results are greyed out and cannot be pressed, while an option you have selected always stays available, and hovering shows how many cards it would add (#21)
- **General:** foil-only and etched-only printings show a shimmer across their card art and a small chip naming the finish
- **General:** while the app is busy, the status line says what it is doing, such as updating card data or downloading an update, and stays quiet for work that finishes in a moment
- **Settings:** Settings has an errors list showing recent failures, newest first, with a count of repeats and a button to clear it (#20)

### Changed

- **Decks:** the old maybe pile is now an ordinary category that can be switched off, and a switched-on maybeboard counts toward deck size like any other category (#18)
- **Sync:** the app spaces out its requests to the card data provider, and after a rate-limit response it waits out the penalty before asking again, even if you restart the app (#20)
- **Sync:** failed sync requests are retried a few times with growing pauses before the sync reports a failure (#20)

### Fixed

- **Search:** searching for a card name puts the card itself above art pieces that share its name
- **General:** card pictures that stall on a slow connection now give up after ten seconds, so the picture spot shows its message instead of staying blank for a minute (#19)
- **General:** card pictures already saved on your computer are no longer downloaded again (#20)
- **Decks:** pictures in the deck builder now load from the copies already on your computer, instead of timing out while pictures in search load fine (#20)
