### Highlights

- **In point and shoot mode the scanner now keeps the last scan's pictures in memory.** They stay until the next scan starts or you leave the scanner, so you can still send a card after it lands. The privacy policy now says so.
- **The scanner waits for three steady frames before it adds a card.** A card that is still moving is no longer added from one lucky frame, which cuts false adds when the camera pans. A card may take a moment longer to be added, and two cards read right at the edge may now go undecided.
- **Shares now refresh from any device that syncs, not only from the desktop app.** A change made on your phone can reach a shared link while the desktop app is off, and the five-minute refresh timer has been removed.

### New

- **Decks:** the deck completion widget can count a planned card as sleeved when you own it in any printing or finish, through a new Theory match setting that is off by default (#1403)
- **Collection:** you can give each stack in your collection its own free-text tags, editable under Edit on the card details and from a new Tags option in the right-click menu (#1406)
- **Wishlist:** you can give each wish its own free-text tags from the card details or the right-click menu, and these tags sync between your devices (#1406)
- **Settings:** a new Integrations section on the desktop app holds the master switch, five opt-in fields that are all off by default, and says when the service is not available in this build (#1409)

### Changed

- **Scanner:** a card is added only after three frames in which it lies still, so a card moving across the view is no longer added from a single frame (#1410)
- **Scanner:** the gold corners are drawn around a found card instead of a green outline, the green flash on landing is now gold, and the orientation dot shows only when the Developer switch is on (#1411)
- **Scanner:** in point and shoot mode the Send scan button stays available between shots, and the privacy policy now says the last scan's pictures stay in memory until the next scan or until you leave the scanner (#1408)
- **Sharing:** shares you have published are refreshed after any device syncs, including a phone with the desktop app off, in the marketplace they were published under (#1409)
- **Web:** shares you have published now refresh from the web app as well (#1409)

### Fixed

- **General:** a home page widget that is dragged stays under the pointer when the page scrolls during the drag (#1404)
- **Decks:** stepping a card's quantity in the deck editor responds at once instead of lagging behind each press (#1407)
- **Collection:** stepping a card's quantity in the collection responds at once instead of lagging behind each press (#1407)
- **Scanner:** the Send scan button no longer vanishes as soon as a card lands in point and shoot mode, so you can send a scan you want to check (#1408)
