### Highlights

- **The Android app and the web app are gone.** This release runs on desktop only. A new, lighter app and web version is planned for later.
- **Update every device before you sync.** Every device in a sync group should be updated before syncing, because older versions cannot write some of the new deck piles and folder deletes.
- **Theory and Actual have their own piles.** Each list of a deck now has its own categories, so a pile made on one tab no longer appears on the other.

### New

- **Search:** a leading minus before a plain word or a quoted name leaves out cards with that name, such as -bolt (#579)
- **Search:** the filter tray can narrow results to regular, borderless or full art cards, and to printings published in foil (#582)
- **Search:** searching finds cards by names their printings carry, such as a flavour name or an English printed name, and quick add adds that exact printing (#586)
- **Collection:** the card details of a copy you hold have an Edit button that lets you change its printing and whether it is foil (#591)
- **Backup:** your collection is copied to the backups folder before an upgrade, and the newest three copies are kept (#585)
- **Decks:** the deck editor shows a floating bar with the main controls once the header has scrolled out of view (#608)
- **Decks:** the Add printing picker has an All tokens option that lists every token printing, including emblems and game markers such as The Monarch (#613)
- **Decks:** a token the deck does not make is marked with a red outline and the words NOT MADE BY DECK (#613)
- **Decks:** Compare has a Tokens view, and its All view includes tokens (#613)
- **Decks:** the managed wishlist has a Tokens mode that files token wishes in a Tokens folder (#613)

### Changed

- **Decks:** Theory and Actual each have their own categories, so renames, reorders and sideboard switches no longer cross over between them (#588)
- **Decks:** managed tokens start at zero, and a token at zero stays out of the deck's token pile but still shows in the tokens band (#613)
- **Decks:** the hide button and the Managed and Hide modes are gone, and every token tile has a Remove printing button instead (#613)
- **Decks:** the option once called Different printing is now called Any printing, and the mismatch mark reads Match when matching printing is off (#613)
- **Decks:** the grid view has its own zoom setting, and each deck view keeps its own scroll position (#584)
- **Decks:** the undo history keeps only the newest 200 steps for each deck (#589)
- **Decks:** sending missing cards to your wishlist from Compare closes the dialog and shows how many wishes were updated (#589)
- **General:** the all printings list shows the newest printing first by default, unless you have chosen another order (#578)
- **Collection:** the collection and wishlist headers are shorter, with Reset all on the filter bar and filter chips shown on the path row (#611)
- **Search:** Reset all sits at the end of the filter bar, and the line of filter chips appears only while something is filtered (#611)
- **Settings:** clearing the image cache is refused while prices, tags or the combo list are downloading (#585)
- **Android:** the Android app is no longer part of this release (#602)
- **Web:** the web app is no longer part of this release (#602)

### Fixed

- **Decks:** a stack stays open while you move the pointer into its right-click menu (#576)
- **Decks:** grid cards are sized in proportion to stacked cards, so their quantity, buttons and labels are no longer oversized (#584)
- **Decks:** when a multi-card drop or Delete is partly refused, the message says how many cards were not changed and why (#589)
- **Decks:** Delete does nothing while a dialog is open over the deck, so it can no longer remove cards you did not pick (#589)
- **Decks:** Ctrl+Z works after you reorder the piles (#589)
- **Decks:** pressing Enter twice in quick add no longer adds a card twice, and text typed during a lookup is kept (#589)
- **Decks:** a switched-on maybeboard is checked for colour identity and the commander's conditions, and counts toward the 100 cards (#590)
- **Decks:** basic lands no longer found in the card database no longer trip the copy limit (#590)
- **Decks:** a rule-break mark sits on the copy it is about, so a parked card no longer shows the problem of its active twin (#590)
- **Decks:** a foil-only card counts as one card whether its Theory or Actual row left the finish unset, so Compare and the wishlist no longer list it as a different printing (#595)
- **Decks:** a foil copy of a foil-only card counts as owned in the deck, instead of showing as missing or unclaimed (#609)
- **Decks:** moving a pile's cards to another pile when you delete it keeps foil and etched copies as foil and etched (#593)
- **Decks:** pressing Escape once in quick add closes the suggestions and keeps the typed text (#610)
- **Decks:** card pictures that were off screen no longer stay on No image after you scroll to them (#613)
- **Sync:** deleting a binder on one device no longer stops sync on a paired device when one of its cards lands on a card that device already holds (#606)
- **Sync:** cards another device files into a folder deleted elsewhere are no longer dropped, so the devices stay in step (#606)
- **Sync:** a new device's cards are held back for less time, and sync no longer asks you to update the app over a change it can read (#574)
- **Search:** an apostrophe inside a word, such as sensei's, no longer swallows the rest of the search (#596)
- **Search:** the filter counts leave out the words you negate, so they match the cards on screen (#596)
- **Search:** after you change your collection or wishlist, the badges on search results update in place instead of reloading every page (#596)
- **Collection:** two quick changes to the collection no longer drop a row from the Owned filter (#596)
- **General:** the app can start after an upgrade was interrupted, rebuilding a card database that cannot be upgraded (#585)
- **General:** a change to your collection or wishlist and its activity entry are saved together, so a failed save leaves neither behind (#585)
