### Highlights

- **Settings moved to Ctrl+9.** Trade and Playtesting now sit before it in the sidebar, so Ctrl+7 and Ctrl+8 open them.
- **The game changer hover preview is gone.** Hovering the game changer count no longer fades the other cards; a Game Changers chip in the label row filters the deck instead.

### New

- **Decks:** a pencil beside the menu on each folder and deck lets you rename it right on its card, without opening the sidebar (#430)
- **Decks:** sending a deck's missing cards to the wishlist now lets you choose a wishlist folder, or make a new one from the same dialog (#443)
- **Sharing:** a collection folder can be published as a read-only link that anyone can open, but publishing is not switched on in this release, so sharing shows a message that it is not available yet (#434)
- **General:** Trade and Playtesting appear in the sidebar as placeholder pages that do not do anything yet (#433)

### Changed

- **Collection:** in grid view the collection's cards form one tall wall that the whole page scrolls, instead of a box with its own scrollbar inside the page (#426)
- **Collection:** copies in a locked folder are counted in your totals and header again, and show a lock mark on the card or table row, while they are still kept out of your decks (#440)
- **Wishlist:** in grid view the wishlist's cards form one tall wall that the whole page scrolls, instead of a box with its own scrollbar inside the page (#426)
- **Wishlist:** the wish tile is redesigned, and the wishlist no longer compares its wishes with your collection (#428)
- **Wishlist:** a wish for a single copy no longer shows its price twice on the tile, while a flagged wish still shows its review note (#432)
- **Decks:** the game changer count stays as a plain number, and a Game Changers chip in the label row narrows the deck to its game changers when pressed, then restores it (#438)
- **Prices:** opening a card on TCGplayer goes to that card's exact product page with the listings filtered to the same finish, instead of a name search, and falls back to a name search when no product is known (#439, #444)
- **General:** the navigation bar uses a camera icon for Scanner (#431)
- **General:** Settings is now Ctrl+9, and Trade and Playtesting take Ctrl+7 and Ctrl+8 (#433)
- **General:** on a phone the bottom bar now holds nine tabs, so some of their labels are cut short (#433)

### Fixed

- **Decks:** a theory deck's Owned figure now counts the cards already in the deck's own box and the copies in your collection that could go into it, instead of showing every card as missing (#441)
- **Decks:** the Game Changers chip's crown sits beside its caption on one line instead of stacking above it and clipping (#442)
