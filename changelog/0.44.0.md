### Highlights

- **Build trades with prices and a balance.** The new Trade view lets you put cards on two sides, see each side's price and what one side owes the other, and save, finish or archive a trade. Trades stay on the device they were made on.
- **Tag your decks in your own words.** A deck can carry any number of tags such as Budget or Meme, set in deck settings and shown on its tile in the deck gallery. Tags you have used before are suggested as you type.
- **Import cards straight into a folder.** The import step can file the cards into a chosen collection folder, or into a new one, and every folder's menu now offers Import.
- **ManaBox binders can be sorted on import.** A ManaBox collection export can be imported so that each binder becomes a folder, and each deck binder becomes a deck.

### New

- **Decks:** you can give a deck your own tags, shown on its gallery tile, and pick from the tags already in use (#883)
- **Decks:** hovering a deck's cover in the deck gallery shows the deck's description above the artist credit (#876)
- **Decks:** a new Unspecified format for decks that are not checked against any rules, which is what a ManaBox deck binder becomes (#882)
- **Collection:** when importing cards you can choose a collection folder to put them in, or create a new folder, and each folder's menu has an Import option (#878)
- **Import & export:** a ManaBox collection export imports its binders as folders and decks, with each card sorted into the right place, and you can turn this sorting off (#882)
- **General:** two-sided cards show a flip button under their quantity controls, and right-clicking one offers flip card (#884)
- **General:** a new Trade view lets you build a trade with two sides, save it, and finish or archive it, and you can reopen or duplicate a trade later (#891)
- **General:** trades are kept on the device they were made on and do not sync to your other devices (#891)
- **Prices:** each side of a trade can be set to a percentage of its market price, from 50 to 150, with the adjusted total shown beside the full one (#891)
- **Collection:** finishing a trade takes the cards you give out of your collection, adds the cards you receive, and can take them off or put them on your wishlist, and it names any deck that would be left short before you finish (#891)
- **Android:** the phone app has a Trade tab, where you can edit a line in a sheet and move it to the other side (#891)

### Changed

- **Import & export:** the import dialog is titled Import cards and its paste box is labelled Card list (#875)
- **Import & export:** files of up to 8 MB can be imported, where the limit used to be 1 MB (#882)
- **Settings:** each release in the version history opens with highlights, followed by what is new, what changed and what was fixed, one line per change (#888)
- **General:** the app starts sooner and typing in a filter redraws fewer rows, because the less-used screens load only when first opened (#886)

### Fixed

- **Decks:** the deck history shows the real message when an action fails, not a generic one (#886)
- **General:** right-clicking a card in the card detail window opens its menu, and menus opened from inside a dialog now show above its dark background (#879)
- **Sync:** a change made on a third device no longer loses to an older change when the two arrive on different syncs (#881)
