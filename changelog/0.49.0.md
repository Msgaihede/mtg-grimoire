### Highlights

- **Next turn is now the Space bar.** On the goldfish board, Next turn moved from N to Space. Enter still plays a focused card.
- **Pop-up messages and a notifications list.** Confirmations and errors now appear as pop-up messages in the bottom-right corner, and a list of them is kept in the left nav.

### New

- **General:** a notifications list in the left nav that keeps a record of confirmations and errors on this device, with an unread count and buttons to remove entries or clear them all (#960)
- **General:** short pop-up messages in the bottom-right corner that confirm actions or explain failures, with a close button on each and a timer that pauses while the pointer is on them (#960)
- **Decks:** more playmat colours for the goldfish board: green, blue, red, purple and slate (#962)
- **Decks:** a card that arrives in any zone of the goldfish board pops once with a gold glow (#962)

### Changed

- **General:** adding, removing, moving and copying cards, folders, decks, trades and settings now raises a pop-up message saying what happened, and a bulk action gives one message instead of one per card (#964)
- **General:** some older inline notices, such as the sidebar drop report, the deck-add refusal line and Link copied, are now pop-up messages instead (#960, #964)
- **General:** a few inline results still appear next to their buttons as well as a pop-up, such as the red failure banners on the deck gallery, collection, wishlist and deck editor pages, and some Settings panels (#964)
- **Decks:** the goldfish hand is a straight row of up to seven cards side by side, with more overlapping when there are more, and the hovered card lifts out of the row (#962)
- **Decks:** lands sit on the left of the opening hand, and later draws go on the right (#962)
- **Decks:** cards on the goldfish table are shown larger, and the command, exile, graveyard and library columns are a little bigger (#962)
- **Decks:** the goldfish card preview opens next to the pointer after half a second, follows the pointer, starts larger, and can be resized with the mouse wheel while it is open (#962)
- **Decks:** the goldfish log is grouped under turn headings with an Opening hand heading for the deal, the current turn is highlighted, and each line has an icon for its kind (#962)
- **Decks:** Next turn on the goldfish board is now the Space bar instead of N, and Space no longer plays a card that still has focus after a click (#962)

### Fixed

- **Decks:** dropping a card back on the goldfish hand no longer plays it on the table underneath (#962)
- **Decks:** the goldfish hand no longer flickers when the pointer rests on a card near its edge (#962)
- **General:** copying a card name or image now says whether it worked, and failed Open on links, opening a new window and saving the start view now show an error instead of failing quietly (#960, #964)
