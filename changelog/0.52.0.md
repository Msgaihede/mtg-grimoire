### Highlights

- **Play Commander against three opponents.** The playtesting tool can now seat three AI opponents at once, and an opponent who loses leaves the game while the others play on.
- **Share a deck with a read-only link.** Supporters can publish a deck as a link that opens as a deck in the app and on the web, with its own preview when pasted into a chat.
- **Shared folders stay up to date.** A shared collection or wishlist now updates itself while the app is open, so you no longer need to press Update now after each change.
- **The Open a shared collection button is gone.** Nothing on the Collection page opens someone else's share any more, so if you have never opened a share you now have no way to start inside the app; share links still open in the browser.
- **More scans now end as not found.** The scanner holds back a crowded answer it cannot confirm, so more scans finish without a card; if that happens, move closer, try again, or search by hand.

### New

- **Decks:** a mana table beside the Next turn button shows which colours of mana your untapped permanents can make, with a row for mana floating in your pool (#1041)
- **Decks:** four-player Commander games in the playtesting tool, with the opponents shown side by side and a choice of one or three opponents on the setup page (#1043)
- **Decks:** undo and redo on the goldfish and playtest boards with Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Y or Ctrl+Shift+Z, and from the top of the game menu (#1045)
- **Sharing:** a deck can be shared as a read-only link from the Share control in the deck editor, viewed as stacks, grid, table or text, and previewed with the deck's cover or commander (#1050)
- **Sharing:** shared collections and wishlists are republished automatically when they change while the app is open, and only changed shares are uploaded (#1044)

### Changed

- **Decks:** the zone cards on the playtesting board are about a fifth larger, and the tables are slightly narrower to fit them (#1029)
- **Decks:** a pile of identical cards on the board shows up to five cards and a larger quantity mark (#1034)
- **Decks:** the playtesting tool takes a question's only possible answer for you and says so in the log, instead of asking you to confirm it (#1040)
- **Decks:** trigger ordering prompts name each trigger by its card and show its picture, instead of two identical Order triggers answers (#1039)
- **Scanner:** a card waiting for you to pick its printing spans the full width of the tray, and each tile's remove button sits in its top right corner (#1037)
- **Scanner:** the scanner holds back an answer that only its picture supports when another printing is nearly as close, so more scans end as not found (#1046)
- **Search:** the Releases label on unreleased cards is a red outlined box instead of a solid red block, and the owned badge sits just above it (#1047)
- **Sharing:** shared collection and wishlist link previews say whether the link shows a collection, a collection folder, a wishlist or a wishlist folder (#1030)
- **Sharing:** an open shared collection checks for updates every 15 minutes, and if a check fails the copy you have stays on screen with a note about the failure (#1044)
- **Sharing:** the Open a shared collection button beside Share on the Collection page has been removed, while the Shared page still offers opening a share (#1048)

### Fixed

- **Decks:** hovering a card in the opening hand on the goldfish and playtest boards now opens its preview (#1031)
- **Decks:** the goldfish's own turn and life changes from game rules now appear in the game log (#1036)
- **Scanner:** the scanner recognises a card that is turned or seen through a sleeve pocket more often, and picks the right card more often (#1046)
- **Scanner:** a card set deeper inside its sleeve or binder pocket is recognised more often (#1052)
- **Settings:** the Check now button no longer stays greyed out after the app starts, and a newer version found by the launch check shows straight away, with a stalled check giving up after 30 seconds (#1042)
