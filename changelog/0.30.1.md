### Highlights

- **Managed wishlists are now off by default.** Each deck starts with its managed wishlist switched off, and the shopping-list folders that the app created automatically are removed. To use one again, choose a view in the deck settings.

### Changed

- **Decks:** deck settings now let you choose which Compare view a managed wishlist follows, with the options Off, All, Missing and Different Printing (#530)
- **Decks:** managed wishlists are off for every deck, and the shopping-list folders created automatically for them are removed when the app starts (#530)

### Fixed

- **Wishlist:** a card a deck already plays in another printing no longer lands on the managed shopping list by mistake (#530)
