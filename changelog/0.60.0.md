### Highlights

- **The scanner plays a sound as each card lands.** A short sound plays when a card reaches the scanner tray, with a bigger jingle for valuable cards. You can switch scanner sounds off and set the price where each tier starts in Settings.
- **A new Wishlist value graph widget.** You can add a home widget that charts your wishlist's value over time, like the collection graph. Its history starts on the day you update.

### New

- **Scanner:** a sound plays as each card lands in the scanner tray, and a more valuable card plays a bigger sound (#1386)
- **Scanner:** from the scan history you can put a past batch of scanned cards back into the tray to file again, and the original batch stays in the history (#1382)
- **Settings:** a scan sounds panel in Settings lets you switch scanner sounds on or off, preview each sound, and set the price where each tier starts (#1386)
- **Wishlist:** a wishlist value graph you can add to the home page, showing your wishlist's value over time with the same splits and ranges as the collection graph (#1388)
- **General:** the playtest board plays a sound for card actions such as drawing, casting, tapping, damage and passing the turn (#1389)
- **General:** a speaker button on the playtest board mutes the table sounds, and the setting is remembered on each device but not synced (#1389)
- **Collection:** a folder heading's row lights up when you hover or focus any of its buttons, including Rename, the menu and the open arrow, not only Add folder (#1380)
- **Collection:** you can drop a card anywhere on a folder's cards in the grid or table to move it into that folder, and the whole folder lights up while a card is over it (#1385)
- **Wishlist:** you can drop a card anywhere on a wishlist folder's cards in the grid or table to move it into that folder, and the whole folder lights up while a card is over it (#1385)
- **General:** during a playtest, hovering a card in a graveyard or exile pile shows a readable preview above the pile, and scrolling the pile scrolls its list rather than the preview (#1383)

### Changed

- **General:** home widgets now fill their boxes: lists use only as many columns as their rows need and grow to fill the card, and the summary shows as many figures as will fit (#1387)

### Fixed

- **Scanner:** in Neural mode, laying a second copy of the same printing on a pile now adds that copy to the tray instead of being missed (#1384)
- **General:** home widgets no longer show scrollbars, clipped tiles, overlapping figures or footers that run into one another (#1387)
