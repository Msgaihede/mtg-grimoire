### Highlights

- **Surge and ripple foils now look like themselves across the app.** Cards printed as Surge Foil or Ripple Foil are drawn in their own style and labelled with their real foil name wherever a card appears, including the wishlist, trades, deck tokens, opened packs, the scanner and shared views.

### Changed

- **Wishlist:** foil wishlist entries draw a Surge Foil or Ripple Foil in its own style and say its real name on the card (#1685)
- **Collection:** opened packs draw surge and ripple foils in their own style and name them on the card (#1685)
- **Decks:** deck tokens and the token art picker draw surge and ripple foils in their own style and name them on the card (#1685)
- **Scanner:** the scanner tray draws surge and ripple foils in their own style and names them on the card (#1685)
- **Sharing:** trades and shared collections, wishlists, decks and the public share page draw surge and ripple foils in their own style and name them on the card (#1685)
- **Android:** the collection, wishlist, search and deck screens on the Android app draw surge and ripple foils in their own style and name them on the card (#1685)
- **General:** the foil shine on cards follows the mouse pointer anywhere in the window, not only when the pointer is over that card (#1687)
- **General:** the foil shine on cards is always at full strength, so it is visible even when the pointer is not over the card (#1687)
