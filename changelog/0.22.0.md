### Highlights

- **A card scanner view is in the app.** The scanner now runs inside the app on its own Scanner entry, and it only settles on a card once enough frames agree.
- **Decks can be virtual.** A virtual deck is a list you track without owning its cards, such as an MTGO or Arena list or a proxy pile, and it is not compared with your collection or wishlist.
- **The numbers on theory marks now say what to do.** A number on a live card now tells you how many to add or remove, so a plus sign means add and a minus sign means remove, which is the reverse of before.
- **The combo list downloads by itself.** Settings no longer has a button to refresh the Commander Spellbook combo list, because it downloads when the app starts, and Local cache can clear it and download it again.

### New

- **Decks:** a virtual deck kind for lists you track without owning the cards, chosen in deck settings and marked VIRTUAL on its tile (#419)
- **Decks:** a red cross marks live cards that the plan does not ask for, and it can be switched off in deck settings (#412)
- **Decks:** the folder tree in the deck gallery can be dragged wider or narrower and collapsed (#418)
- **Decks:** missing cards can be added to your collection from the deck editor in one press (#411)
- **Decks:** pressing the game changer count fades every card that is not a game changer, and hovering over it does the same (#423)
- **Settings:** the colour of the cross for cards not in the theory list can be changed under theory marks in Appearance (#412)
- **General:** an Open on EDHREC link sits in a card's options and in the right-click menu, and the marketplace link is named after the one chosen in Settings (#405, #409)
- **General:** a Combos row on a card's options lists every Commander Spellbook combo that card is in, with filters for combo size and for combos you own every piece of, and each combo shows its pieces, steps and mana (#421)
- **General:** the combo list can be searched by any card name, and each combo opens only when you press it (#424)
- **Scanner:** a Scanner entry before Settings in the app's menu opens the camera, with a verdict on each card it sees (#415)

- **Decks:** the number on a live card's theory mark now says how many to add or remove, not how far over or under the plan it is (#404)
- **Decks:** the deck gallery is redesigned, with mana colour bands fused to the deck tiles and folders drawn to match them (#407)
- **Decks:** the grid view draws each card the way the stacks view does, with the same frame, marks and size (#410)
- **Decks:** in the grid view, the sideboard, maybeboard and switched-off piles come after your own columns rather than above them (#410)
- **Decks:** the table view grows with the page instead of scrolling inside it (#410)
- **Decks:** the table view lists its piles in the same order as the stacks view (#413)
- **Decks:** the view picker lists Stacks first, then Grid, Table and Text (#410)
- **Decks:** the tokens and emblems row is drawn at the same size as the stacked cards and follows the deck's zoom, and the art picker opens larger (#422)
- **Decks:** the tokens and emblems row now sits above the deck stats (#422)
- **Decks:** the game changer mark is now a small crown printed before the count on each card, instead of its own ribbon (#423)
- **Decks:** the bracket readout no longer asks you to fetch the combo list from Settings, and the download shows its progress (#408)
- **Settings:** there is no button for the combo list in Settings, and Local cache has a button that clears it and downloads it again (#408)
- **Scanner:** a card is decided by a vote across frames, and once decided it stays until you reset or the card leaves the camera (#415)
