### Highlights

- **Foils look different.** Regular foil has its old sheen back, mana foil has its own pattern of rings, and every foil is softer. The light now moves across cards at an angle instead of following your pointer.
- **Set completion counts every set.** Sets released after Phyrexia: All Will Be One now show their progress, and copies numbered past a set's printed run are counted too. The widget now sorts by recently changed by default.
- **A match finish switch in the scanner.** A new switch stops the scanner matching printings that were not made in the finish you chose, such as a foil-only printing when you file nonfoil. It is off by default, so nothing changes until you turn it on.

### New

- **Scanner:** a Match finish switch in the Filters that leaves out printings not made in the finish chosen under Finish, off by default (#1697)
- **General:** settings in the set completion widget to count against printed set size or every printing, and to show all sets or only chosen ones (#1696)
- **General:** toggles in the set completion widget for progress bars, complete sets, and token and promo sets (#1696)
- **General:** a footer in the set completion widget, from three cells wide, that shows how many sets are in scope and how many are complete (#1696)
- **Search:** clicking a set in the set completion widget opens that set on the search page (#1696)

### Changed

- **General:** regular foil has its old sheen back, and its bright band turns to each card's own angle (#1693)
- **General:** mana foil has its own pattern of rings with mana symbols in them, instead of plain foil (#1693)
- **General:** foil drawings reach the card's edge, and every foil is less bright, so frames no longer flare to white (#1693)
- **General:** ripple foil shows a fixed set of rings that grow and fade as the light crosses them, instead of drifting pictures (#1693)
- **General:** the foil light no longer follows the pointer; each card shifts a little as the pointer crosses the window, and the card under the pointer shifts more, each at its own angle (#1693)
- **General:** the set completion widget sorts by recently changed by default instead of nearest complete (#1696)
- **Collection:** the finish buttons under a card's details, and the right-click menu on its picture, name the kind of foil, such as Set as surge foil or View as mana foil (#1695)
- **Decks:** the right-click finish rows on a deck card, and the message shown after a change, name the kind of foil too (#1695)

### Fixed

- **Decks:** a deck of more than a hundred foil cards no longer lags when the pointer moves across it (#1693)
- **General:** sets released after Phyrexia: All Will Be One now show their progress, instead of a dash and a place at the bottom of the list (#1696)
- **General:** copies numbered past a set's printed run, such as showcase, borderless, extended art and Commander reprints, now count towards its progress (#1696)
