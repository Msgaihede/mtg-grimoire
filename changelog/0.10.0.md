### Highlights

- **Cards in a deck can now be set to foil.** A deck card names its finish, so a foil copy is priced at its foil price and kept apart from the regular copies in the same pile.
- **Decks can be given a game type.** The game type narrows the list of formats offered when you pick a format for the deck.
- **Release notes are in Settings.** The update panel now shows the notes for the current release and every earlier one as readable text, and each one stays closed until you open it.
- **The deck card menu no longer explains greyed-out commander and companion rows.** The reason those rows are greyed out is still shown in the validation panel, and the menu rows are narrower as a result.

### New

- **Decks:** a deck card can be set to foil with the foil button on the card, and the deck prices it at the foil price (#110, #112)
- **Decks:** a deck can be given a game type, which filters the formats offered in the format picker (#108)
- **Decks:** piles that are switched off appear in their own row beneath the sideboard (#107)
- **Decks:** piles on the desk can be dragged past their neighbours to reorder them, picked up by their heading (#111)
- **Settings:** the update panel shows the release notes for the current release and every earlier one, each opening when you press it (#105)

### Changed

- **Decks:** the four quick drop zones at the top of the deck are larger with clearer labels, so they are easier to spot while dragging a card (#102)
- **Decks:** when a deck is too wide for its piles, the sideboard sits beneath the deck on the left instead of at the far right (#104)
- **Decks:** the dialogs that open over the deck editor share one background darkness (#99)
- **Decks:** the deck card menu rows for setting a commander or companion are narrower, because the explanation no longer sits beside greyed-out rows (#100)

### Fixed

- **Decks:** clicking a card held in two piles marks only the row you clicked, so one press no longer lifts the card clear of two piles at once (#103)
- **Decks:** piles near the edge of the deck no longer have their drop highlight cut off while you drag (#101)
- **Decks:** the cover shown for a deck updates straight away when you replace its custom cover, instead of showing the old picture (#99)
- **Collection:** a row whose quantity is set to zero no longer shows a per-copy price above a total of zero dollars (#99)
- **General:** when you zoom a card, its marks, caption and buttons grow and shrink with it (#106)
