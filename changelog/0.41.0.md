### Highlights

- **Back up everything as a ZIP.** Settings can now save all your collection, wishlist, decks, notes and layout to one ZIP file and restore it later. A restore replaces what is on the device, so keep the copy the app saves first.
- **Use the app in a browser.** The web app at mtg-grimoire.app builds its own card database on the first visit, opens offline after that, and syncs with your other devices.
- **Full pages on a phone.** On a phone, the collection, wishlist, decks, search and settings are now complete, and you can make changes on them, not just look.
- **The Android app can change your data and sync live.** The Android app can now make changes, not only read them, and it stays connected to your other devices while it is open.
- **On mobile data the Android app waits.** Large card and price downloads wait on a metered connection until you choose Always or Not now. Not now is the default.

### New

- **Decks:** on a phone each deck shows as a cover in its folder, and opens to one column with its piles, its side panel and its checks (#787)
- **Decks:** on a phone you can add and remove cards, move them between piles, set a commander, labels, printings and finishes, write deck notes and to-do lists, with an undo line after each change (#790)
- **Decks:** on a phone you can create a new deck, or make one from a pasted list, straight from the Decks page (#802)
- **Collection:** on a phone the collection is shown as shelves you can fold open and drill into folders, with its own filters (#791)
- **Collection:** on a phone you can change a copy in your collection or a wish in your wishlist: its count, condition, finish, printing, folder, or remove it (#794)
- **Collection:** the Not sorted shelf can be collapsed by its name and has an Add folder button (#830)
- **Wishlist:** on a phone the wishlist is shown as shelves like the collection, with managed wishes marked by their deck and its own filters (#792)
- **Search:** on a phone search has a filters sheet, and a card page that shows prices, what you own, printings, legality, oracle tags and combos (#785)
- **Search:** the card page on a phone links to Scryfall, EDHREC and your marketplace (#807)
- **Import & export:** on a phone you can import cards into a deck, and export a deck or your collection (#793)
- **Settings:** on a phone Settings is split into six sections that open one at a time (#788)
- **Scanner:** the scanner has start and stop buttons, so you can pause card recognition while the camera stays on (#826)
- **Backup:** you can export all your app data as a ZIP and restore it from one (#828)
- **Sync:** on a phone you can pair with another device by scanning its code or typing the six digits (#820)
- **Sync:** the web app can sync with your other devices, and changes arrive on their own while it is open (#818, #823)
- **Android:** the Android app can change decks, collection, wishlist and notes, not only read them (#796, #797)
- **Android:** the Android app stays connected to your other devices while it is open, so changes arrive without a press (#819)
- **Android:** the Android app pads its screen around the status bar, cutouts and keyboard, so nothing sits under them (#798)
- **Android:** the Android app saves files through the system save dialog, picks decklist files ending in .dec and .dek, and opens web links in your browser (#798)
- **Android:** on a metered connection the Android app asks before large downloads, and defaults to not now (#799)
- **Web:** the web app runs the whole app in a browser, and on its first visit it downloads card data and builds the card database (#805, #806, #808, #811)
- **Web:** the web app works offline after its first visit, and asks you before it replaces itself with an update (#809)
- **Web:** the web app can copy text, open links, save files and pick files from your computer or phone (#807)
- **Web:** the web app can be installed to a home screen, with its own icon and colours (#807)

### Changed

- **Settings:** the phone and web versions of Settings leave out updates, backup, the data folder and the start view, which do not apply there (#788)
- **Backup:** before an update installs, the app saves a full backup first, and stops if that backup fails (#828)
- **Import & export:** the export button reads Save file, and the status line says Saved or Downloading to show what happened (#801)
- **Search:** the phone's Filters button counts only the filters, not the search text, and Reset all keeps the search text (#801)
- **Web:** the web app redirects plain http addresses to https, and www addresses to the main address (#815)
- **Web:** the web app no longer uses Ctrl+Shift+N, so the browser can open a private window with it (#789)
- **General:** on a phone the tab bar becomes a side rail on windows 600 pixels wide and wider (#789)
- **General:** text you are typing can be lost when a window is resized across 1024 pixels wide (#789)

### Fixed

- **Decks:** the add button in a deck's search names the pile a card will really go to, and the collection tab files Auto adds into the pile its rule picks (#813)
- **Decks:** a new install's Decks page offered nothing for making a first deck, and now offers New deck and From a list (#802)
- **Search:** a card search said no cards matched before any cards had downloaded, and now says the card download is running, with a progress bar in the header (#803)
- **Search:** the card page's figures for your collection update after an add, without reloading the app (#810)
- **Web:** reloading the web app during the first card download no longer leaves you on the already open in another tab screen (#810)
- **General:** a first start no longer records a false error about the card database (#801)
- **General:** the card view's side arrows no longer squeeze its layout in windows from 900 to 1061 pixels wide (#795)
- **General:** going back closes a card view that was opened on the desktop layout, and leaves no empty history entry (#789)
- **General:** on a phone, buttons and controls are at least 44 pixels tall, so they are easier to tap (#820)
- **Sync:** a device that left its group or was removed stops its live connection, joining another group connects that group, and a removed device is told at once (#825)
- **Sync:** a large catch-up from other devices is applied in parts, so the app stays responsive while it works (#829)
- **Sync:** a phone refreshes its pages when another device's changes are applied, not only after a restart (#820)
- **Sharing:** a removed device can no longer regain sharing access by redeeming a code with an old sign-in (#827)
- **Android:** leaving the Android app with the back gesture no longer ends it with an error (#804)
