### Highlights

- **Decks can now be grouped by label or shown as one full-deck pile.** Under Group by you can now sort a deck into stacks by label, or put the whole deck in a single stack so that a sort covers every card at once.

### New

- **Decks:** group a deck by its labels, with a no label stack first and then one stack per label sorted by name (#464)
- **Decks:** group a deck as one full-deck stack, so sorting covers every card in the deck, with the commander at the top and the sideboard, maybeboard and switched-off piles kept separate on the right (#464)

### Fixed

- **Decks:** the mana sources count in the deck's mana panel now shows only the colours that the deck's spells ask for, while colourless is always counted (#463)
