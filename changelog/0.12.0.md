### Highlights

- **You can now clear your collection, wishlist, decks or local cache from Settings.** Clearing the collection also releases every deck's claim on owned copies, and the three irreversible clears make you type `Confirm` first. Clearing the cache only removes images that download again.
- **Import and export now work for any list of cards.** You can export your deck, a deck category, your collection or your wishlist in seven formats, choosing which fields go in the file. You can import into a deck, a new deck, the collection or the wishlist, including from CSV files.
- **The wishlist can be shown as a wall of card art, and it opens on that wall.** Each tile is one wish, showing what you already own of it, what the missing copies would cost and whether it needs review.

### New

- **Decks:** live cards that the deck's plan also asks for carry a mark in their top-right corner, so the proxies standing in for cards you have not bought yet can be told apart (#138)
- **Decks:** a tag can be given any colour you pick, not only one of six (#141)
- **Search:** search results can be sorted by mana value and by release date, from a sort picker that works in both the table and the grid view (#148)
- **Wishlist:** the wishlist can be shown as a wall of card art, one tile per wish, and each tile has a single button that opens a panel for changing that wish (#137)
- **Settings:** a page lets you clear your collection, wishlist, decks or local cache, with the three irreversible clears asking you to type Confirm first (#146)
- **Import & export:** you can export your deck, a deck category, your collection or your wishlist in seven formats, choosing which fields the file carries (#152)
- **Import & export:** you can import a list of cards into a deck, a new deck, your collection or your wishlist, from pasted text or a file including CSV (#152)
- **General:** the previous and next arrows in the printings window now step through search results, your collection and your wishlist as well as through a deck (#144)

### Changed

- **Decks:** the quantity box on a card in a deck's stack view is a quarter smaller so it no longer crowds the card (#129)
- **Decks:** the editor's count of differing cards is replaced by a Compare button, and the comparison matches cards by printing (#131)
- **Decks:** the rule break mark on a card in the stack view moves to its bottom-left corner (#138)
- **Decks:** the Tags dialog opens on making a new tag, with its explanation in a header line and suggestions from your other decks in their own section (#141)
- **Decks:** clicking a tag's colour swatch opens a colour picker to change it (#141)
- **Decks:** choosing New tag from a card's tag menu opens a dialog to name the new tag instead of a text field in the menu (#141)
- **Wishlist:** the wishlist opens on the wall view rather than the table (#137)
- **Import & export:** the export format and the chosen fields are remembered separately for each list (#152)
- **General:** the printings window is much wider, so more printings fit on screen at once (#135)
- **General:** the set picker in the printings window is the same one the search uses, and it offers only the sets the card was printed in (#135)
- **General:** the legality badges under a card's prices now sit under a Formats heading like the printings list does (#133)
- **General:** the app opens at 1920 by 1080, or at 1280 by 720 when the screen is too small for that, centred on the screen (#142)
- **General:** the window draws its own title bar instead of the one Windows provides (#147)
- **General:** hover hints across the app now appear as the app's own tooltips instead of the plain pop-up that Windows and Linux draw (#149, #151)

### Fixed

- **Decks:** the count of differing cards no longer counts each difference twice, so a deck of a hundred cards no longer reads as a hundred and fifty differences (#131)
- **Decks:** a planned foil copy is no longer matched by a regular copy in the live list, and each row in the comparison shows which finish it is (#132)
- **Decks:** the stack view no longer grows its own scrollbar when a card is open, and the deck no longer jumps under the pointer (#136)
- **Decks:** cards in switched-off piles now show a rule break outline and badge when they break the format's rules (#143)
- **Search:** search results collapsed into groups now sort by price and by name in the order you asked for (#148)
- **General:** a disabled control's hint no longer swallows the Tab key, and Tab now opens the hint of a control that is disabled (#151)
