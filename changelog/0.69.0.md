### Highlights

- **The search filter bar has been redesigned.** It fits its width, moves less-used controls into a Filters popup, and replaces Show all printings with Stack printings, which works the other way round: on means one tile per card.
- **Each kind of foil now looks like itself.** A surge, ripple, etched, silver or other special foil has its own shine on the collection wall, in decks and in the scanner, instead of one shared sheen.
- **A THEORY ONLY deck shows its plan on its tile.** The Decks page shows the card count and colour bar of the theory list until the first card goes into the Actual list.

### New

- **Decks:** the deck editor's search column can be walked with the arrow keys, and a card opened from it can be stepped through in its details and printings (#1676)
- **Decks:** a card in the deck editor's search column shows how many copies are already in that deck, in its own mark beside the collection count (#1676)
- **Search:** the less-used filters sit in a Filters popup that opens over the results and closes on Escape or a press outside it, and the Filter bar moves Grid/List and the sort into a View cell in that popup when the window is narrow (#1682)
- **Search:** Tokens & extras in the Include cell shows tokens and other extras, replacing the Any card choice in the format picker, and picking a format turns it off again (#1682)
- **Prices:** in the Price movers widget each chosen card can be set to Any finish or to one finish it comes in, so a foil can be followed on its own, and cards chosen before this stay on Any finish (#1677)
- **General:** card details show one button per finish a card comes in, labelled View as or Set as, and right-clicking the card picture offers the other finishes too (#1680, #1681)
- **General:** foil is drawn as its own kind, such as traditional, etched, surge, silver, ripple, galaxy, rainbow or textured, and its shine follows the pointer while the card stays flat (#1683)

### Changed

- **Decks:** a deck built from a theory list shows that list's card count and colour bar on its tile, and the count turns red only when the plan does not fit the format (#1678)
- **Decks:** the right-click menu on a deck card lists each other finish as its own Set as row in place of a Finish submenu (#1680)
- **Search:** the Show all printings toggle is now Stack printings, which is on by default and shows one tile per card; turn it off to see every printing (#1682)
- **Search:** the stated filters under the bar sit on one line that scrolls sideways, and the sort is a single control with a fixed width (#1682)
- **General:** wording across the app is shorter and plainer, errors begin with Couldn't, retry buttons say Try again, and sync means your own devices (#1684)

### Fixed

- **Collection:** a Surge Foil copy on the collection wall is labelled Surge Foil instead of plain Foil (#1683)
