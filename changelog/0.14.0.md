### Highlights

- **Tags are now shared by every deck.** A tag is one app-wide name with one colour, so recolouring it changes it everywhere and two tags cannot share a name. Remove takes a tag off one deck's cards, while Delete removes it from every deck.
- **Deck undo history is cleared.** This update discards the undo history for decks, so changes made before it cannot be undone. The audit record of past changes is kept.
- **Your decks can count as your collection.** A new Settings option makes the Collection page and every owned figure read the cards in your decks rather than the ones you added by hand. Your own list is kept and comes back when you switch the option off.
- **Wishlist folders.** The wishlist can now be sorted into nested folders, and wishes can be dragged in and out of them.

### New

- **Collection:** a Settings option makes the Collection page and every owned figure count the cards in your decks rather than the ones you added by hand, and your own list comes back when you switch it off (#208)
- **Decks:** in the Compare dialog you can untick rows you do not want, then send the rest to the wishlist in one press, with select all or none (#202)
- **Decks:** the Compare dialog can show only missing cards or only cards you already hold as another printing, and says when a row is split between the two (#202)
- **General:** the navigation sidebar can be collapsed to a column of icons and stays that way after a restart (#196)
- **General:** the app has its own logo (#199)
- **General:** the Windows installer shows the app's licence before it installs (#198)
- **General:** the printings dialog names the kind of special foil a printing is, such as surge or halo, and has a Special foil chip (#184)
- **General:** in the card details pane, split, planar and flip cards can be turned to read the right way up (#193)
- **General:** a meld card in the card details pane shows its other half, and you can open that half (#193)
- **General:** the card size you set with ctrl and the scroll wheel is remembered for each part of the app after a restart (#191)
- **Import & export:** a checkbox in the MTG Arena export leaves out cards MTG Arena does not have, and is off until you tick it (#204)
- **Search:** the card search box understands Scryfall tag syntax, so you can search by what a card does or what its art shows (#205)
- **Wishlist:** wishes can be sorted into nested folders, and Add to Wishlist can file a card straight into one, while a new wish still lands at the top level unless you file it (#211)
- **Wishlist:** a wish can be dragged into a folder and back out again, including wishes for any printing (#211)
- **Wishlist:** a wish's printing can be changed to a specific printing, or back to any printing, from the all printings dialog (#211)

### Changed

- **Decks:** tags are now shared by every deck, so recolouring a tag changes it everywhere and two tags cannot share a name, and the right-click menu lists only the tags in use on this deck with More tags opening the rest (#194)
- **Decks:** Remove takes a tag off this deck's cards and keeps the tag, while Delete removes the tag from every deck (#194)
- **Decks:** undo history for decks is cleared by this update, so changes made before it can no longer be undone (#194)
- **Decks:** the left and right arrow keys step one card at a time through the whole deck, across pile boundaries, while the up and down keys scroll the page (#189)
- **Decks:** the piles in a deck sit closer together from side to side (#188)
- **Decks:** opening a card no longer pushes the deck aside, and its details open over the search column when opened from a deck or over the deck when opened from search (#200)
- **Decks:** the search column in the deck editor reopens by default, and it stays open or closed as you left it (#200)
- **Decks:** the corner mark on stacked cards in a deck is mirrored and narrower, so it covers fewer mana symbols (#187)
- **Wishlist:** a wish sent from the Compare dialog is for the exact printing and foil finish in the plan rather than any printing (#202)
- **Search:** clicking a tag in the Tags page list now adds it to the filter or removes it (#201)
- **General:** the card size steps in even 10 percent stops from 50 to 200 percent (#191)
- **General:** the desktop and taskbar icon is the transparent mark, so it no longer sits on a black square (#207)
- **Settings:** the wishlist wipe in Settings also deletes the folders (#211)

### Fixed

- **Collection:** owned counts update as soon as a deck changes, rather than after a delay or a restart (#210)
- **Collection:** switching the deck driven collection off no longer lets the Collection page change a row you cannot see (#210)
- **Decks:** deck shortage marks stay accurate while decks drive the collection, and switching it off restores the right claims (#210)
- **General:** the window's close button and title bar stay clickable above the sync screen and dialogs (#203)
- **General:** search boxes clear when the clear button is pressed instead of just bouncing (#190)
- **General:** the sidebar's icons and labels no longer jump or overflow while it collapses (#206)
- **Search:** oracle tag search on the Tags page finds tags again in databases upgraded from an older version (#195)
