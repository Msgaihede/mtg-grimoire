### Highlights

- **Adding, moving or removing many cards at once is now quick.** A picked set of hundreds of cards is handled as one fast action, and a busy indicator shows while longer work runs.
- **Only the card under your pointer moves its foil shine now.** Cards you are not hovering keep a still shine, so the slow idle movement is gone. Etched cards are also softer, so their rules text stays readable.

### New

- **General:** a busy line in the header, a small pill where the receipt appears and a progress cursor show when work takes more than a moment (#1699)
- **Collection:** adding a set of cards to the collection offers Undo on that list's page, as bulk moves already do (#1699)
- **Wishlist:** adding a set of cards to a wishlist offers Undo on that list's page (#1699)
- **Search:** selecting all results with Ctrl+A is quicker, and the busy indicator shows while the rest of a long list loads (#1699)

### Changed

- **Decks:** adding or moving many cards in a deck is one undo step, and the undo button names the batch by its count, such as Undo — Moved 50 cards (#1699)
- **Decks:** a drop that both adds new cards and moves existing ones offers no Undo (#1699)
- **Wishlist:** a picked set of existing wishes dropped on a folder moves entirely or not at all, as bulk moves in the collection do (#1699)
- **Sharing:** adding cards to a wishlist on a shared collection adds the rest when one card is refused and then reports the refusal (#1699)
- **General:** the home feed shows one line for a bulk add, such as Added 300 cards to your collection, instead of one line per card (#1699)
- **General:** cards you are not hovering keep a still shine, and only the hovered card moves its light, with the slow idle movement gone (#1698)
- **General:** etched cards have a softer grain and glare, and the rules text stays clear across the card (#1698)

### Fixed

- **Decks:** adding, moving, removing or deleting hundreds of cards at once no longer freezes the app (#1699)
- **Decks:** a very large bulk action no longer stops with a database busy error (#1699)
- **Collection:** adding, removing, moving or changing the quantity of a single card in the collection is quicker (#1699)
- **General:** the app no longer slows down under the pointer when many foil cards are on screen (#1698)
