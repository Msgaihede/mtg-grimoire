### Highlights

- **Folders are now shelves.** The collection and the wishlist show every card in one wall under nested folder headings, and Flatten has been removed.
- **Tokens and emblems get their own pile.** A deck's tokens look and move like its other piles, can hold several printings, and can be set to Managed or Hide.
- **Sync no longer drops changes.** A device that was offline while the group changed now receives what it missed, and changes from a device on a newer version are held until this device updates, which the Sync panel now says.
- **Undo is stricter.** Undo and redo now check the deck before writing, and an undo that cannot be applied is removed from the history rather than blocking every later undo.

### New

- **Decks:** the Add printing picker on the Tokens and Emblems band adds another printing or finish of a token, with a search box (#562)
- **Decks:** deck tokens can be set to Managed, which keeps them as a pile, or Hide, which shows only the band, from the band header or Deck settings (#562)
- **Decks:** the Collection tab of the deck search panel has an Any card option, so owned tokens can be found there (#542)
- **Decks:** the deck completion home widget shows each deck's owned and wanted cards and what the missing ones cost at your marketplace, and opens the deck when pressed (#543)
- **Collection:** the collection value graph home widget shows the collection's value over time, split by total, card type, colour or set (#537)
- **Wishlist:** the wishlist savings home widget lists pinned wishes that have a cheaper printing, biggest saving first, and opens optimise when pressed (#543)
- **Search:** the coming soon home widget lists unreleased sets, how many of their cards are previewed and how many are already in your decks (#543)
- **General:** the to review home widget counts scanned cards waiting, flagged binder entries, wishes and deck cards, and opens each list when pressed (#543)

### Changed

- **Decks:** the tokens and emblems pile is drawn with the same card faces as the deck's other piles, and each pile heading is now one row with its copy count in a pill (#542)
- **Decks:** the tokens and emblems pile can be dragged to a new place in the right-hand rail, or moved with the arrow keys, and the move can be undone (#542)
- **Decks:** a token is never counted as a deck card, so adding one from the search panel files it under Tokens and Emblems, and cutting the last card that makes a token removes the token too (#562)
- **Decks:** every change to a deck's tokens can be undone, and theory marks on tokens show the number planned for each printing (#562)
- **Decks:** undo and redo are stricter: redo only reverses the last undo, the Redo button is greyed for any other step, and an undo that cannot be applied is removed from the history (#538)
- **Collection:** folders are shown as shelves, every card below the current folder in one wall grouped under each folder's heading, with Flatten removed (#565)
- **Collection:** a folder cannot be deleted while a folder inside it is locked, and the menu says why (#539)
- **Wishlist:** wishlist folders are shown as shelves in the same way as the collection's, and can be reordered by dragging their headings (#565)
- **Prices:** a price typed with a lone dot before three digits, such as 1.500, is refused as ambiguous, and the reason appears under the price box and in the import preview (#539)
- **Sync:** the Sync panel tells you when a device in your group runs a newer version of the app and asks you to update this device to receive its changes (#572)
- **Sync:** a device cannot be removed while its joining is not yet finished on the other devices, and the app says so (#540)

### Fixed

- **Decks:** the cover art grid in deck settings grows to the height of the fields beside it, instead of stopping short behind a scrollbar (#533)
- **Decks:** cards on the Collection tab of the deck search panel can be dragged into a deck pile, landing as one copy (#535)
- **Decks:** undo no longer brings back a card you cut, leaves copies behind, or overwrites a change made in another window, and a folder deleted since no longer blocks undoing a rename forever (#538)
- **Collection:** a plain copy in the Collection tab no longer shows a foil shine (#562)
- **Import & export:** renaming a deck by letter case alone keeps its plain-text files instead of deleting them (#539)
- **Prices:** a price such as 4,50 is read as 4.50 instead of 450 on import, and 1,500 is read as 1500 in the price boxes instead of 1.5 (#539)
- **Sync:** a device that was offline when another device joined the group now receives the changes it missed, including deletions (#540)
- **Sync:** a device leaving the group no longer stops the other devices from syncing (#540)
- **Sync:** a device whose reply to a key change was lost can sync again instead of stalling (#540)
- **Sync:** local edits are no longer left out of sync after the app was closed unexpectedly during a sync (#540)
- **Sync:** a removed device can no longer make the remaining devices leave the group (#541)
- **Sync:** changes from another device are no longer dropped when one of them cannot be applied, and changes from a newer version wait until this device updates (#572)
