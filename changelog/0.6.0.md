### Highlights

- **Prices now come from the marketplace you choose.** Settings lets you pick TCGplayer, Cardmarket, Card Kingdom or Mana Pool, and every price in the app follows that choice. Card trader is listed but cannot be chosen yet.
- **The collection and wishlist show one currency instead of two.** The side-by-side dollar and euro totals are gone. The collection header and the wishlist now show the marketplace you picked, with its currency in the label.
- **You can import a decklist.** Paste or open a list from Moxfield, Archidekt, Arena or MTGO, check the preview, then add it to a new deck or merge it into or replace an existing one.

### New

- **Import & export:** you can paste or open a decklist and see a preview before anything is added, with any card names that were not recognised listed by line number (#32)
- **Decks:** importing into a deck that already has cards lets you merge the list in or replace the deck's cards with it (#32)
- **Prices:** settings lets you choose the card marketplace that prices are quoted from, with TCGplayer as the default, Cardmarket, Card Kingdom and Mana Pool (#31, #33)
- **Prices:** card trader appears in the marketplace list but cannot be selected yet (#31, #33)
- **Prices:** card Kingdom and Mana Pool prices are downloaded the first time you choose them and refreshed at most once a day while they stay chosen (#33)
- **Prices:** the card pane shows the price of each finish at the marketplace you have selected (#33)

### Changed

- **Collection:** the collection header shows one value in the selected currency instead of dollar and euro values side by side (#31)
- **Wishlist:** the wishlist shows one still-to-buy total in the selected currency instead of two (#31)
- **Prices:** on Cardmarket an etched card shows an em dash rather than a price, because no euro price exists for it (#31)
- **Decks:** a card added without choosing a pile is filed into the pile that matches its card type, and a card you drop onto a pile still goes there (#22)
- **Decks:** the quick add field shows no category name when set to automatic, and each card's add button names the pile it will go to (#22)
- **Decks:** deck cards are drawn as the whole card with its art and printed name visible, and the quantity badge sits over the mana cost (#22)
- **Decks:** deck creation opens as a dialog in the middle of the screen instead of a panel docked to the side (#32)
- **Decks:** a deck stack opens the card under the pointer after a short pause and closes it after a brief delay, so passing over cards no longer opens them (#24)
- **General:** panels, popups and status messages animate in and out, and the animations stop when your system asks for reduced motion (#24)

### Fixed

- **Decks:** moving down a deck stack selects the card you aimed at instead of one several cards lower (#24)
- **Decks:** a card you open in a deck stack stays behind the cards that are still moving, so the stack keeps looking stacked (#26)
- **Decks:** quick add no longer puts every card into Commander on a new deck (#22)
- **Decks:** the quantity field of a deck card no longer shows browser spin arrows on top of the number (#22)
