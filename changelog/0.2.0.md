### Highlights

- **Build and check decks.** Decks are new in this release: build a deck from your collection, see which cards you own and which are missing, and check the deck against its format's rules.
- **Search and track your collection.** Search every card, see its details and prices, and add it to your collection or wishlist.
- **The card database downloads itself.** The app downloads the full card database and checks for updates once a day, showing progress as it goes.

### New

- **Decks:** create decks with a name and a format, then rename, duplicate, archive or delete them
- **Decks:** open a deck to see its cards split into main deck, sideboard, maybe and commander, change quantities, move cards between them, pick a cover and rename the deck
- **Decks:** see which cards in a deck you own, and send the ones you are missing to your wishlist
- **Decks:** deck cards follow Scryfall renames and removals in the same way collection entries do
- **Decks:** each deck is checked against its format for deck size, copy limits, banned and restricted cards and singleton exceptions
- **Decks:** commander decks are checked for a legal commander, partner pairs, colour identity and companions, with an advisory power bracket estimate
- **Decks:** a stats panel shows the mana curve, colour and land pies, card types, average mana value, price and how many cards you own
- **Decks:** a search panel beside the deck finds cards to add, and it folds away to a narrow strip when there is no room
- **Decks:** drag cards from search, the collection, the wishlist and card details into a deck's zones, or onto the Decks entry in the sidebar
- **Decks:** a deck card can be swapped to another printing of the same card from the card details
- **Decks:** each card in a deck zone shows its picture as a thumbnail beside its name and count, and the compact text list is still available
- **Search:** search cards by name and filter by format, colour, set and mana value, with a button that resets every filter
- **Search:** results load as you scroll and can be shown as a grid of card pictures or as a table
- **Search:** click a card to see its details, its faces, its other printings grouped by artwork, and its prices
- **Search:** click any other printing in the card details to open that printing
- **Search:** hover a printing in the card details to see its picture beside the list
- **Search:** the pictures for the first page of results are fetched ahead of time so they appear straight away
- **Collection:** keep a collection by adding cards, changing how many copies you own, setting condition, finish and price, and removing them
- **Collection:** see your collection as a list or grid with its card count, unique cards and total value, and filter it
- **Collection:** add a card to your collection or wishlist from the card details, the grid or the results table
- **Collection:** when Scryfall renames or removes a card, your entries move to the new printing, and any that cannot be matched are flagged for review
- **Collection:** the pictures for your collection are fetched in advance so they appear straight away
- **Wishlist:** keep a wishlist of cards and printings you want, with a count of what is still to buy, and see on search results which cards you own or wish for
- **Wishlist:** the wishlist can be filtered to entries needing review, and shows prices in euros
- **General:** a sidebar with Search, Collection, Wishlist and Decks, a refresh button, and a progress line during card database updates
- **General:** card pictures are kept on your computer once downloaded, so they load quickly after the first time
- **Sync:** the app downloads the card database and checks for updates at most once a day, in the background at launch, with progress shown

### Changed

- **Collection:** setting a card's copies to zero keeps the entry with its details, and only Remove deletes it
- **Search:** search results are ranked by how well the name matches, and the total shows 5,000+ cards when there are more than that
- **Sync:** the card database takes less disk space and returns freed space after each update
- **General:** the app runs one copy at a time, so opening it again no longer starts a second copy
- **Decks:** the card-type bars stop at a readable width

### Fixed

- **Sync:** a failed download no longer replaces the card database with an empty one, and a failed update leaves the existing one working
- **Sync:** a download that is cut short or resumes from the wrong place is no longer accepted
- **Sync:** sync failures are shown in the app and retried on the next launch instead of waiting a day
- **Sync:** the first-run screen gives a way out when the first download fails
- **Sync:** the card database is reported as unreadable rather than shown as empty when it cannot be read
- **Sync:** editing the collection no longer waits for a card database update to finish
- **Sync:** a half-finished card database update is cleaned up when the app starts
- **Sync:** lists and sets refresh when a card database update ends, even when it then fails
- **Search:** searching for a name with an apostrophe or hyphen finds the card
- **Search:** results already loaded stay on screen when a later page fails, and a new search starts at the top
- **Search:** a rarity name with no colour stays readable in results
- **Search:** a picture that fails to load is retried later rather than shown as missing for good
- **Search:** the printings list shows paper printings only
- **Search:** the set picker stops at 64 sets and says why when you reach that limit
- **General:** pressing Escape closes one open panel at a time and returns focus to where you were
- **General:** a card picture that Scryfall has not finished producing is no longer kept as that card's picture
- **General:** pictures that hit Scryfall's rate limit wait and retry instead of stalling the list
- **Collection:** a text filter no longer shows entries for cards the database has dropped
- **Collection:** a set filter now includes entries whose card has left the database
- **Collection:** a foil-only printing can no longer be added as nonfoil from search
- **Collection:** stray badges and empty corners no longer appear on collection and search tiles
- **Wishlist:** a foil wish is no longer filled by a non-foil copy
- **Wishlist:** owned counts on the wishlist update straight away after a collection change
- **Wishlist:** sending missing cards to the wishlist from a deck updates the hearts on search results straight away
- **Decks:** the card count in the deck header matches the format's size rule
- **Decks:** commander checks accept partner oathbreakers and legendary Spacecraft in Tiny Leaders, and the bracket estimate reads mass land denial and extra turns more accurately
- **Decks:** deck steppers no longer lose presses, and a row menu opens upward when it would run off the bottom
- **Decks:** the missing-to-wishlist button says when there is nothing new to add and cannot add the same cards twice
- **Decks:** the delete question keeps focus where you left it, and a deck cover that cannot load says so
- **Decks:** a click while a new deck is being created no longer closes the form
- **Decks:** dragging cards works on Windows
