### Highlights

- **Play a test game against an AI opponent.** Pick your deck, an opponent deck from your own decks and a difficulty, then play a game where the computer controls the opponent. Later changes let you stop to respond, aim attacks and watch the opponent's turn replayed.
- **Some phone settings messages are gone.** The danger zone, cache and paired messages no longer appear on the phone's settings page, though a pairing that has expired or was refused still shows a line.
- **Refused edits are reported once.** A refused edit to the collection, wishlist or deck now shows only as a pop-up message, not also as a banner at the top of the page, except where the banner is the only report.

### New

- **Decks:** the playtest page lets you choose your deck, an opponent deck from your own decks and a difficulty before you play, and the opponent's hand and library are shown only as counts (#973)
- **Decks:** during a playtest you can stop the game to respond to the opponent's spells and abilities, after blockers are declared, or at steps you choose on either player's turn (#974)
- **Decks:** during a playtest you can aim each attack at the opponent or at one of their planeswalkers or battles, and pick several cards at once for scry, surveil, discard and search (#974)
- **Decks:** during a playtest the opponent's turn is replayed step by step, and you can skip the replay with the skip button, Space or Escape (#974)
- **Decks:** during a playtest, combat shows arrows from attackers to what they attack and from blockers to their attackers, with damage and life changes popping up (#974)
- **Android:** on the phone, a confirmation of most changes now appears as a pop-up message above the bottom bar and the undo line, with a close button, instead of as a line on the page (#966)
- **Android:** the phone's header has a bell that opens your notifications, with a dot when there are unread ones (#966)

### Changed

- **Search:** an unreleased card shows a small red chip with its release date in the bottom-left of its picture instead of a large diagonal stamp, and its owned badge moves up so the two do not overlap (#970)
- **Decks:** the command zone, exile, graveyard and library on the goldfish table are drawn half again as large (#972)
- **Decks:** the playtest tables are a little taller and the cards on them are noticeably wider (#974)
- **Android:** the danger zone, cache and paired messages no longer appear on the phone's settings page, though an expired or refused pairing still shows a line (#966)
- **General:** a refused edit to the collection, wishlist or deck is reported once as a pop-up message, and the page banner is kept only for changes that have no other report (#967)

### Fixed

- **Decks:** on the goldfish board the card preview closes as soon as the pointer leaves the card, wherever that card is, and no longer lingers on the next card (#969)
- **Decks:** on the goldfish board, when a turn ends with more than seven cards in hand you now choose which cards to discard, instead of the newest cards being discarded without asking (#971)
