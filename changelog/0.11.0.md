### Highlights

- **Browse every printing of a card.** A card's printings open in one popup that you can filter.
- **Arrow keys move through cards.** The arrow keys now walk the card grids, a deck's piles and the printings popup, including after you click a card.

### New

- **Import & export:** you can export a deck in a TCGplayer format (#117)
- **Search:** open every printing of a card in a popup that you can filter (#119)
- **General:** move through card grids, a deck's piles and the printings popup with the arrow keys (#120)

### Changed

- **Decks:** the format check is now a green tick when the deck is legal and a red warning when it is not, with a count bubble for the number of issues, and it no longer shifts the layout when you switch between Live and Theory (#116)
- **Decks:** the quick drop targets above a deck are sized to cover the deck's name bar and spread wider on large windows (#115)
- **Decks:** the sideboard rail stays at the right edge of the deck view at every window width and zoom level (#118)

### Fixed

- **Decks:** setting a card as foil no longer deselects it, and the card panel's close and foil buttons work afterwards (#113)
- **Decks:** the quick drop targets no longer leave the bottom of the deck's name bar showing underneath them (#115)
- **General:** dialogs taller than the window now fit it and their contents scroll (#117)
- **General:** the arrow keys now work after you click a card, not only after you move to one with them (#121)
