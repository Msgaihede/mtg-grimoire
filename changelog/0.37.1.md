### Changed

- **Scanner:** the scanner's panel now shows where the collector number was read from, the size of the image it was read from, and says when the reading matches the printing the scanner chose, and the title readout lists each front face once (#728)

### Fixed

- **Scanner:** the scanner now reads both the card's title and its collector number before it adds a card, and waits for a sharp frame first, instead of often adding cards with neither read (#728)
- **Scanner:** a card whose title is only partly read now still matches the cards that title names, instead of matching nothing (#728)
- **Scanner:** blurry collector numbers are now matched against the known printings of the card, so letters misread as digits no longer stop the card from being identified (#728)
- **Scanner:** the collector number is now found on short lines that were previously missed, because the artist credit no longer drowns them out (#728)
- **Scanner:** cards lying almost flat now have their corners placed on the card's real edges instead of sitting inside the card (#728)
- **Scanner:** a card held upright is no longer shown sideways once the scanner has locked onto it (#728)
