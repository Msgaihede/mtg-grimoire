### Highlights

- **Goldfishing is now a full game.** Goldfishing plays like a playtest against a passive opponent, with real phases, combat, prompts, targets and arrows.
- **Some goldfish controls have gone.** The Next turn button and the discard dialog are gone, and hand edits in a goldfish game are refused unless the sandbox is on.
- **Foretell is available.** You can foretell a card in hand for two mana and cast it later from exile.

### New

- **Decks:** the playtesting setup marks each deck with a check when the rules engine plays every card in full, or a warning when some cards are played only in part, and lists those cards under each seat (#1084)
- **Search:** the Universe filter has Beyond and Within buttons in every card filter, on the search page, in the deck builder, and on the collection and wishlist pages (#1087)
- **Search:** the search box understands is:universesbeyond and is:ub, as Scryfall does (#1087)
- **General:** a foretold card can be cast from exile on a later turn, and a card in exile or the graveyard with a way to cast it, such as flashback or escape, is cast from there (#1086)
- **General:** a sandbox setting lets you change any card the table shows in your hand, and it can be switched on in either a goldfish or an opponent game (#1085)
- **General:** the playtesting setup page offers a Goldfish choice alongside one opponent and three opponents (#1085)
- **General:** the goldfish's life plate reads Dead on your turn with the turn number, highlighted, once its life reaches zero (#1085)
- **General:** each spell or ability on the stack has a dotted arrow to every card, spell or player it targets (#1089)
- **General:** a card the rules engine plays only in part has a gold border on the table, in hand and in the card preview, with the parts it leaves out named in the preview (#1094)

### Changed

- **Prices:** the price movers widget and its settings note now say prices are remembered from the day you choose a card, and say when the chosen time window will have a starting price (#1082)
- **General:** goldfishing is now a playtest against a goldfish opponent, so it uses the same turn flow, stage button, combat and prompts as a full game (#1085)
- **General:** turn numbers in goldfishing now follow the game, so your fifth turn is Turn 9, and a spell with a choice of targets asks you to pick one instead of being put down unpaid (#1085)
- **General:** hand edits in a goldfish game are refused unless the sandbox is on, and discarding down to hand size is now a pick on the table (#1085)
- **General:** an attached Aura, Equipment or Fortification is tucked behind the creature it is attached to, with its name peeking out above, instead of sitting loose in the back row (#1088)
- **General:** the cards in the command, exile, graveyard and library columns beside the playtest tables are drawn at the size of the table cards in a tall window, and stay at their small size in a short one (#1095)

### Fixed

- **Prices:** a card chosen in the price movers widget gets its price recorded at once, so the widget no longer shows no history for days after you choose it (#1082)
- **General:** when casting a spell that can be paid for in two ways, such as overload, the two answers are labelled with their cost names instead of both reading Choose alternative cast (#1090)
- **General:** a keep-within-total-power sacrifice asks which creatures to keep, with the total power shown and over-the-cap picks refused, instead of offering two identical answers (#1091)
- **General:** a card played for no mana in the sandbox is cast as a real spell, so its cast and enter triggers happen, instead of being placed onto the battlefield (#1097)
- **General:** when one ability triggers more than once at the same time, each trigger is told apart by what set it off, in target questions, trigger orders and on the stack, instead of showing identical text (#1092)
