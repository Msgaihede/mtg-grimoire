### Highlights

- **Shares can be published from the app now.** Publishing a collection share works in this release, where earlier builds refused it. Withdrawing a share now also removes its copy from the server.
- **Live sync connects again.** Changes now reach your other devices within seconds rather than about a minute, and a case where pulls stopped is fixed.
- **A pairing baseline no longer loses rows silently.** A device paired with another could quietly miss some rows when a baseline arrived in parts or out of order. This is fixed.

### New

- **Scanner:** a Clear all button on the review tray empties every waiting scan after you confirm (#743)
- **Scanner:** a Reset evidence button beside the match progress bar lets every reader make the scanner start over on a card (#746)
- **Scanner:** each scanned card in the review tray shows the price of one copy at the marketplace you chose (#747)
- **Scanner:** a Next card needing a decision button in the review tray jumps to the next card waiting for a printing or a finish (#744)
- **Scanner:** a detail wait slider in the scanner's developer controls sets how long it pauses before reading a card's fine print (#765)
- **Sharing:** you can publish a share link to your collection from the app, which earlier builds refused (#756)
- **Decks:** a View all toggle on the decks page shows every deck from every folder side by side (#767)

### Changed

- **Scanner:** some cards take a few frames longer to appear in the tray, because the scanner waits for a clearer read before deciding (#755)
- **Scanner:** the Reset evidence button no longer appears in the developer match panel and is only beside the progress bar (#746)
- **Sync:** writing a note or a deck while a sync is running is no longer held up until the sync ends (#771)

### Fixed

- **Scanner:** a minimised scanner window stops the camera and the card reading, and lets another window take the scanner (#749)
- **Scanner:** the scanner no longer adds two cards when you move quickly from one card to the next, and takes back an early guess the card does not confirm (#755)
- **Sync:** live sync connects through the relay, so changes reach your other devices in seconds rather than about a minute (#754)
- **Sync:** devices whose wishlist held tokens stopped pulling changes from the others, and they pull again (#754)
- **Sync:** a baseline sent in parts or after other changes no longer silently loses rows on a paired device (#780)
- **Sharing:** a withdrawn share is removed from the server, and a device removed from the group can no longer publish or withdraw shares (#745)
- **Decks:** pasting plain-text lines into a deck's to-do list makes each line its own to-do (#757)
- **General:** the activity list on the home page shows changes made on other screens straight away (#753)
- **General:** reordering sticky notes with pinned first keeps the order you dragged them into (#753)
- **General:** the combos panel says when a card could not be read, rather than saying it is not in the database (#753)
- **General:** the app's update check stops repeating once the screen that started it has closed (#753)
