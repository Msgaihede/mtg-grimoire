### Highlights

- **The app is now called MTG Grimoire.** If you installed the installer version, it now appears as a separate app, so copy your data folder across to keep your collection. Portable copies are not affected.
- **Sort tables by clicking their column headers.** Search results, your collection and your wishlist can now be sorted by their column headers, and in search Shift-clicking adds a second or third sort.
- **The app can tell you when a newer version is out.** A button on the ribbon and a panel in Settings check for updates, and where the app can replace itself, the new version can be installed from inside the app.

### New

- **Search:** you can sort search results by clicking a column header, and Shift-clicking a second or third column sorts by more than one
- **Collection:** you can sort your collection by clicking its column headers, including the value column, which sorts by the total worth of your copies
- **Wishlist:** you can sort your wishlist by clicking its column headers, and the cost column sorts by what is still left to buy
- **General:** a button on the ribbon appears when a newer version is available, and where the app can replace itself it can download and install the update from inside the app
- **Settings:** there is an updates panel that checks for a newer version, shows the download size, and offers to restart once the download has been verified

### Changed

- **General:** the app is called MTG Grimoire, and an installer copy of the old version shows up as a separate app, so its data folder needs copying across to carry your collection over
- **General:** card pictures load faster, with no delay between each one
- **Collection:** the sort menu reads Custom when the list is sorted by a column header that it has no option for
- **Search:** Reset all leaves the sort alone, so clearing your filters does not change the order of the results

### Fixed

- **Search:** the set picker no longer hides behind table headers or pushes the whole app sideways when it opens near the window edge
- **General:** card pictures no longer show the previous search's art next to new card names when a search changes
- **General:** after an update on Windows the new version opens reliably instead of closing silently, and the restart button is no longer greyed out
