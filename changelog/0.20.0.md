### Highlights

- **The card view is now a pop-up.** The card detail panel that sat beside your list is replaced by a centred window. Printings, legality, oracle tags and card text open in windows stacked on top of it.
- **Two undocumented keyboard shortcuts no longer work.** Ctrl+Shift+Y no longer redoes and Shift+Delete no longer removes a card. Both only worked by accident.
- **The coloured mark on a deck card is now a label.** Scryfall's tags keep their name, and the deck mark is called a label everywhere in the app. Older CSV files still import their labels.
- **Lock a collection folder to set its cards aside.** A locked folder's cards are left out of your collection lists and what you can build, while still counting as yours. Deleting a locked folder is refused.

### New

- **Decks:** you can pull copies you already own from your collection into a deck, choosing between copies filed in different folders (#373)
- **Decks:** a deck card you are short of has a collection submenu to quick add the missing copies to your collection, optionally taking them off a matching wish, or to pull copies you already own (#381)
- **Collection:** you can lock a folder so its cards are left out of your collection lists, the deck collection search and what you can build, while they still count as owned (#375)
- **Wishlist:** you can optimise a folder of wishes so each pinned wish moves to the cheapest printing at your marketplace, previewing every change before you apply the ones you pick (#380)
- **General:** a keyboard shortcut map opens from a button in the title bar or with F1, listing the shortcuts for the page you are on, and Ctrl+1 to Ctrl+6 jump between the main pages (#379)
- **General:** the card pop-up can be turned a quarter turn and flipped, and shows legality, oracle tags and card text in their own windows (#382)

### Changed

- **Decks:** a Commander deck with no Game Changers, mass land denial, extra turn chains or combos now reads as bracket 2 instead of bracket 1 (#343)
- **Decks:** the card search in the deck builder counts only the copies that deck can use, so copies sleeved into other decks no longer count and the owned filter follows the same count (#378)
- **Decks:** you can only file copies into a deck's folder for a card the deck already plays, and the card menu greys out decks that do not play the card (#372)
- **Decks:** the coloured mark on a deck card is called a label throughout the app, including in deck settings and dialogs (#371)
- **Decks:** deck tiles show Theory + Actual or Theory only and no longer show a badge for a deck with one list, and the clear actions and history now say actual list (#374)
- **Collection:** the quantity control on collection cards is a vertical stepper on the card's right edge like the deck builder's, and is smaller in the table (#376)
- **Collection:** creating and renaming a folder happens inside the folder tile on the collection and wishlist walls instead of in a strip above them (#366)
- **Wishlist:** the quantity control on wishlist cards is a vertical stepper on the card's right edge like the deck builder's, and is smaller in the table (#376)
- **Settings:** settings are split into a left rail of groups with one panel shown at a time, and a search box finds a setting by a word such as a service name (#368)
- **Import & export:** the Replace option in the import dialog says that any copies you own go back to Recently removed when it replaces a live list (#345)
- **Import & export:** exported CSV files use Label and Label colour for the coloured marks, and older files still import their labels (#371)
- **General:** the card detail window is a centred pop-up in place of the side panel beside your list (#382)
- **General:** a drop target is outlined with a thin line inside its edge and a dragged card is shown see-through (#369)
- **General:** every foil finish uses the same sparkle icon, and the tooltip and label still name the treatment (#370)
- **General:** Ctrl+Shift+Y no longer redoes and Shift+Delete no longer removes a card (#379)

### Fixed

- **Decks:** importing over a deck's live list no longer leaves copies you own out of view and unavailable to other decks, because they go back to Recently removed (#345)
- **Decks:** theory cards no longer show a red 0/1 or say you own 0 of their copies (#367)
- **General:** a dropdown's own list can be scrolled with the mouse or keyboard without the picker closing (#346)
- **General:** a card picture that never arrives is requested again, so it no longer stays as an empty box until you move the mouse (#347)
- **General:** focus outlines no longer appear around a whole panel after you click it and press a key (#384)
