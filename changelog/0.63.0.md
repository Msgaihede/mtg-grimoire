### Highlights

- **A new Draft & Packs page on the desktop app.** You can draft against bots or with friends in a pod, and open boosters to see how they compare with the average value. Drafted decks are built from your pool and do not take cards out of your collection.
- **Play a multiplayer game of Playtesting with friends.** Start a lobby on the Playtesting page and share its code. Friends join with their own decks for a duel or a four-player game, and the computer can fill any empty seats. Starting a lobby needs a supporter membership.

### New

- **General:** a new Draft & Packs page on the desktop app, with its own button in the left-hand menu (#1418)
- **General:** draft against bots in quick, sealed, cube, Commander, Winston or pod formats, picking cards from boosters and building a deck from the pool (#1418)
- **General:** pod drafts where friends join with a six-letter code, the person who starts it chooses the format, and bots fill any empty seats; only supporters can start a pod (#1418)
- **Prices:** open a booster or sealed product from any set and see its value against the average, including the chance to beat a price you type; the set's card data is downloaded the first time you pick that set and kept for seven days (#1418)
- **General:** multiplayer Playtesting, where the person who starts a lobby shares its code and friends join with their own decks to play one game together; if a friend leaves, the game waits for them or the computer can take their seat (#1421)

### Changed

- **Scanner:** the gold corners drawn around a scanned card are now a fine two pixels wide on every screen and square at the bend instead of rounded and thick (#1412)
- **Decks:** while a drafted deck is open, its editor hides the card search, quick add and import, and offers save, export and discard instead (#1418)

### Fixed

- **General:** when a spell makes you sacrifice several creatures in Playtesting, you now choose exactly which ones with a counter showing your progress, instead of picking from a few odd sample sets with no count (#1420)
