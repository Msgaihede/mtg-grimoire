### Highlights

- **On a phone, Back and the tabs work differently.** The Back button now closes open panels first, and the tabs follow Android's bottom navigation: Back from any tab goes to Search, and Back from Search leaves the app.
- **Supporters can send a scan to help improve the scanner.** While a camera scan is running, a supporter can press Send scan to send the last few seconds of camera frames with the exact printing and finish they choose, and nothing is sent unless they press it.

### New

- **Decks:** you can set a deck's cover picture from a card's right-click menu in the deck editor by choosing Set as deck image (#895)
- **Scanner:** supporters can press Send scan during a camera scan to send a short run of frames with the printing and finish they choose, to help improve the scanner (#890)
- **Android:** pinch on a card's picture to zoom it, drag it with one finger when zoomed, and double tap to zoom in or reset it (#900)
- **Android:** pinch on a card list to change how many columns it shows, and the choice is remembered (#900)
- **Android:** drag the header of a panel down to close it (#900)
- **Settings:** the Android and web apps have an Updates section that shows the version you are running, its release date, and the notes for every release up to it (#904)

### Changed

- **Android:** the Back button now closes an open panel, such as filters, a card's actions or a Settings panel, instead of moving the page underneath or leaving the app (#900)
- **Android:** the tabs now work like Android's bottom navigation: Back from any tab goes to Search, Back from Search leaves the app, and tapping the current tab scrolls it to the top (#900)
- **Android:** on a phone, pinching the screen no longer zooms the whole page, and pulling down no longer refreshes it (#900)
- **Sync:** the code shown for pairing a new device is smaller and quicker to scan (#898)
