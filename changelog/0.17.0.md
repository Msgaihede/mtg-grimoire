### Highlights

- **Your collection's top level now shows only cards kept outside any folder.** Cards filed in a deck live in that deck's group, so for anyone who has built decks the top level is empty until you press Flatten, which shows every card captioned with its folder.
- **Adding from the search tab in a deck now always adds the card as needed.** It no longer takes a copy from your collection. To use a copy you own, add it from the Collection tab, which asks which copies to move.
- **You can drag folders to reorder them or nest them.** This works in the decks sidebar and on the folder cards of the decks, collection and wishlist pages.
- **You can drag a card in the collection's picture view into a folder.** When one tile stands for several copies, a dialog asks which copies to move.

### New

- **Decks:** the deck page's gallery tiles and folder cards can be zoomed in and out (#240)
- **Decks:** folder cards on the decks page have a right-click menu for rename, move and delete (#248)
- **General:** folders in the decks sidebar, the collection and the wishlist can be dragged to reorder them, or dropped on another folder to sit inside it (#248)
- **Collection:** dragging a card tile in the picture view into a folder works, and a tile holding several copies opens a dialog to choose which copies move (#244)
- **Collection:** the card menu's Add to list also offers your decks, and where you have folders it shows a greyed Recently removed entry that says why it cannot be used (#244)
- **Collection:** the collection's picture view shows a separate tile for each finish of a card, each with its own price (#249)

- **Decks:** the list that was called Live is now called Actual, and the deck editor's tabs read Theory and Actual (#243)

- **Decks:** a card the plan asks for a different number of times shows the difference, such as +2 or -8, in place of the tick (#245)
- **Search:** the Filters button stays plain until a filter is on, then turns gold, and it keeps a thin border so it still reads as a button (#241, #242)
- **Collection:** the collection and wishlist filter rows now match the search page, and their filter trays gain finish and condition, with rarity and price added to the collection (#239)
- **Wishlist:** the wishlist's filters gain colour, set, mana value, rarity and format (#239)
- **Collection:** import and export are now a joined pair of icons above the list, next to the totals (#239)
- **Collection:** the collection's top level shows only cards not filed in a folder, and Flatten shows every card captioned with its folder (#246)
- **Collection:** New folder is the first tile in the folder area, and Flatten sits in the filter row beside the picture and table buttons (#239, #246)
- **General:** every card list opens as pictures, and it remembers whether you last chose pictures or a table (#239)
- **General:** option lists across the app open in one common panel that stays on screen, with a search box in longer lists (#247)
- **Prices:** cards show their price beneath the picture, and a card's price matches the finish you are looking at (#249)
