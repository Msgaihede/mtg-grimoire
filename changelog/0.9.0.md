### Highlights

- **Undo and redo in the deck editor.** `Ctrl+Z` now steps back through your recent changes to a deck, and `Ctrl+Y` steps forward again.
- **Decklists import with their own headings, and decks export in six formats.** A decklist's own category headings now become piles, and a deck can be exported in six formats, including Arena, Archidekt and a sectioned Moxfield layout.
- **Where new cards go by default has moved.** The choice that used to sit on the search panel is now in the deck settings, and it is saved with each deck.

### New

- **Decks:** in the deck editor, `Ctrl+Z` undoes your last change to a deck and `Ctrl+Y` redoes it, covering card, pile and tag changes (#90)
- **Decks:** while a card is being dragged, four drop zones appear across the top of the deck: Auto, New category, Maybeboard and Sideboard (#82)
- **Decks:** dragging a card already in the deck onto Auto files it again by what it does, and a drop that cannot move it says so (#86)
- **Decks:** a pile's right-click menu has Clear stack, which asks first and then removes every card from that pile (#80)
- **Decks:** a card's right-click menu has Remove card, which takes it out of the deck without a confirmation (#80)
- **Import & export:** a decklist's own category headings now become piles, and each imported card is filed in the pile its file named (#93)
- **Import & export:** an import can be set to skip creating a pile it would otherwise add (#93)
- **Import & export:** the deck export offers six formats, including Arena, Archidekt and a sectioned Moxfield export, and says what each one leaves out (#93)
- **Import & export:** a whole deck can be exported from the deck editor's header (#93)

### Changed

- **Decks:** where a new card goes by default is now set in the deck settings and saved with the deck, rather than on the search panel (#81)
- **Decks:** the Grid, Stacks, Table and Text choice is now a drop-down rather than four buttons (#75)
- **Decks:** the plus, minus and count controls on a card in a deck are twice as large (#77)
- **Decks:** the mark on a card you have just added is now gold with a glow, and it fades after five seconds instead of ten (#78)
- **Decks:** dropping a card on Auto when the app cannot place it now files it in a pile called Uncategorized instead of leaving it where it was (#92)
- **Search:** a collapsed search result now shows its number of printings as the words `12 printings` in its corner (#87)

### Fixed

- **Decks:** in Stacks view, a pile that wraps to a new row starts under the pile above it instead of leaving a blank band across the desk (#83)
- **Decks:** a deck card for a printing sold only in foil or etched now shows its price instead of a dash, and counts in its pile's total (#88)
- **Decks:** the deck editor no longer adds a second scrollbar to the whole window or leaves blank space under the app (#89)
