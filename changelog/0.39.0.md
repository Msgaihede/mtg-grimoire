### Highlights

- **The scanner now leaves cards it cannot check for foil as Unknown instead of guessing.** Unknown cards stay in the tray in the accent colour until you pick their finish. Add to collection still adds every card with a known finish.

### New

- **Scanner:** the scanner tells foil from non-foil by the star or dot beside a card's collector number (#732)
- **Scanner:** cards printed in only one finish get that finish straight away, without the scanner checking the picture (#732)

### Changed

- **Scanner:** the Defaults finish setting has a new Detect option, now the default, which fills in the finish from the card when it can, and a finish you have already set is kept (#732)
- **Scanner:** a card whose finish the scanner cannot tell is marked Unknown in the tray, shown in the accent colour in both the list and grid views (#732)
- **Scanner:** the Add to collection button adds the cards with a known finish and leaves Unknown cards in the tray, with the count of cards that still need a finish shown on the button (#732)
- **Scanner:** picking a printing for an Unknown card also sets its finish when that printing exists in only one finish (#732)

### Fixed

- **Scanner:** a card whose collector line was misread by the camera now matches to the right printing instead of none (#732)
