### Highlights

- **Folders can now hold rules.** Collection and wishlist folders can flag cards that do not match a rule, such as a price range, colour or finish. Rules only flag cards and never stop one being filed.
- **Scanned cards are kept in a history.** Cards added or archived from the scanner are saved to a scan history on the desktop app. On the phone you can archive the tray and open the history too.
- **Pulling from the collection now asks first.** Pulling a card from your collection always opens a dialog, even when only one copy is available, so one more press is needed to confirm.
- **Uncertain scans now wait for you to confirm.** A scan that matches only loosely is put in the tray as a choice for you to confirm, instead of being added straight away.
- **Cards can be added to the scanner tray by hand.** A card the scanner cannot read can be searched for and added to the tray yourself.

### New

- **Decks:** pressing the red missing figure on a deck's ledger filters the deck to the cards you are short of, and pressing it again shows the whole deck (#1008)
- **Wishlist:** wishlist cards show how many copies you already own, and pressing that count shows those copies in the collection (#1013)
- **Collection:** collection and wishlist folders can have rules, such as a price range, colour or finish, that flag the cards which do not match (#1015)
- **Scanner:** a skip promos switch in the scanner's filters stops it answering with promotional printings (#1010)
- **Scanner:** a card the scanner cannot read can be searched for and added to the tray by hand (#1011)
- **Scanner:** past scans are kept in a history that you can open, rename or delete, and cards can be archived there without being added to the collection (#1016)
- **Android:** the phone's scanner menu has Archive and a scan history view (#1021)

### Changed

- **Decks:** pulling a card from the collection always opens a dialog that shows which copy will be used, so you confirm with one more press (#1000)
- **Decks:** the missing figure on a deck card is red when no copy is owned anywhere else and gold when copies sit elsewhere, and its tooltip lists where (#1012)
- **Decks:** the opening hand fills the board in goldfishing and playtesting, with larger cards (#1006)
- **Decks:** label filter chips in the deck editor show each label's colour (#998)
- **Scanner:** the scanner stops reading cards while the Send scan dialog is open, so a card left under the lens cannot be added in the meantime (#997)
- **Scanner:** more binder scans are read correctly, and a loose match is shown as a choice to confirm rather than accepted outright (#1018)
- **General:** the coloured mana line only shows its colours while something is syncing and is grey when idle (#1003)

### Fixed

- **Collection:** copies of foil-only and etched-only cards now show their price instead of a dash (#1005)
- **Decks:** tokens in playtesting and goldfishing use the printings chosen for the deck (#1009)
- **Scanner:** a scanned card can no longer be filed in a finish its printing was never made in (#1017)
- **General:** tooltips on the desktop app are no longer cut off by the title bar (#996)
