### Highlights

- **Point and shoot scanning.** The scanner can now read one card per press instead of reading continuously, so a card passed under the camera is less likely to be filed by accident. It sits alongside the existing start and stop scanning.
- **Every main page has a title bar.** Each page now opens with a heading, its figures and a rule beneath it, and sub-pages show a trail of where you are.
- **Scans can be sent with no card in the picture.** Send scan now lets you report a picture where no card is visible, and scans you send keep more detail.

### New

- **Scanner:** on the desktop app a Point & shoot switch on the scan bar turns Start and Stop into Scan card, where one click reads until one card lands and then pauses by itself (#1371)
- **Scanner:** on a phone the options sheet has a new Scanning page, and with Point & shoot turned on, holding the Scan button reads cards and stops as soon as you let go (#1371)
- **Scanner:** the Send scan dialog has a No card in the picture switch for reporting a picture where no card is visible (#1372)
- **General:** playtesting shows a small picture of each card the game only plays in part, beside its name in the list (#1367)

### Changed

- **General:** every main page now has a title, a line of its figures and actions, and a grey rule underneath, without taking up extra height (#1358)
- **Decks:** folders and the deck editor show a trail such as Decks › Expensive Decks, and each part of the trail takes you back (#1358)
- **Settings:** sub-pages of Settings show a trail such as Settings › Updates (#1358)
- **Scanner:** the scanner panels have section headings, and below about 1700px wide the scan bar's controls wrap to a second line sooner (#1358)
- **Scanner:** with Point & shoot on, the Send scan button is offered only during a shot (#1371)
- **Scanner:** scans you send keep the picture at up to 1920 pixels wide, so they take more data to upload, and changing this setting starts the scan again (#1372)
- **General:** app updates are checked against a published digest before anything is installed, and a failed check leaves the running app as it was (#1362)
- **General:** the playtesting highlight for a card played only in part is now red instead of gold, so broken behaviour reads as broken (#1367)

### Fixed

- **General:** a creature wearing an aura, equipment or role can be clicked to choose it as an attacker, and a lifted attachment no longer covers the creature it is on (#1370)
- **General:** a source that makes mana of any colour now labels each option with the colour it adds, such as black, instead of the same label for every option (#1369)
- **Scanner:** a second printing or art laid on a card the scanner has already identified is now added as another copy, though two identical copies still need the card lifted away or the quantity stepper used (#1368)
