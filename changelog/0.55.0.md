### Highlights

- **Find anything from the title bar.** a quick search box now sits in the middle of the desktop app's title bar, so you can jump to a deck, a collection or wishlist entry, a card or a setting without leaving the page you are on.

### New

- **Search:** a quick search box centred in the title bar of the desktop app opens with `Ctrl+K` from anywhere and shows decks, collection and wishlist entries, cards and settings as you type (#1100)
- **Search:** clicking a collection or wishlist result in quick search takes you to that card's folder with the search filled in to find it, and clicking a card from all cards opens a search for its exact name (#1100)
